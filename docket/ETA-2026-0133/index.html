  • Claim: C1–C2: Schedule A rationale

    Underlying Assumption: Individualized testing is legally required, or categorical findings cannot be reliably maintained.

    Negation Test: If valid categorical certification is permitted and reliable shortage measures exist, the legal and methodological reasons for wholesale repeal require reconsideration.

    Counterfactual Test: Persistent shortages in a specialty or rural service area can coexist with uncertainty about a national occupational list.

    Adversarial Interpretation: Employers might emphasize national shortages to avoid evidence about local availability; the rule appropriately preserves local recruitment.

    Procedural Implication: Request the statutory distinction between categorical certification and exemption, and a reasoned response to concrete methods submitted in the earlier RFI.

  • Claim: C3: two-year no-layoff filing condition

    Underlying Assumption: A relevant layoff up to two years earlier reliably indicates currently available workers or circumvention.

    Negation Test: If affected workers have moved, retired or declined recall, a categorical bar does not necessarily identify available labor.

    Counterfactual Test: A genuine recovery after a temporary contraction can create a vacancy with no available former employee.

    Adversarial Interpretation: Related entities or changes in SOC classification could obscure layoffs; the third-party and successor provisions reduce some evasion opportunities.

    Procedural Implication: Compare six-, twelve- and twenty-four-month screens with documented recall and current-availability checks.

  • Claim: C4–C5: uniform recruitment is effective and inexpensive

    Underlying Assumption: Broader, longer recruitment increases qualified responses at limited incremental cost.

    Negation Test: If additional channels attract no qualified applicants, gross newspaper savings alone do not demonstrate proportionality.

    Counterfactual Test: An experienced specialist position, a household position and a rural nursing vacancy may have different search channels and hiring urgency.

    Adversarial Interpretation: Employers might choose low-visibility free websites unless equivalence, monitoring and normal-practice requirements are enforced.

    Procedural Implication: Request channel-by-occupation recruitment outcomes, paid-channel baselines and 30/60/90-day sensitivity analyses.

  • Claim: C6–C8: savings and streamlined filing

    Underlying Assumption: Calculations reconcile and employers can follow a consistent calendar.

    Negation Test: An unreconciled baseline or contradictory filing windows undermines predictability even if modernization remains beneficial.
