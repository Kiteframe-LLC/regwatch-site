Step 10 — Regulatory Incentives

The notice creates incentives for states, Tribes, local governments, and regulated parties to submit current monitor, exceptional-event, boundary, and contribution evidence quickly. It may also incentivize strategic emphasis on data treatments that move an area across the 9.0 threshold or alter the scope of a nonattainment area.
