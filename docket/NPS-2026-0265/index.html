What This Document Does

The NPS proposes to open Castle Mountains and the original Craters of the Moon monument to hunting under state and federal law. Hunting already occurs in adjoining lands and the Craters preserve; those existing opportunities are distinct from this opening. Proposed protections include a ban on hunting listed species, a Castle Mountains watering-place buffer and intended mine buffer, and short-range weapons plus a northern closure at Craters. Superintendents could impose additional protective restrictions. These are proposed changes, not current permission to hunt.
