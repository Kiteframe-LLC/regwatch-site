## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes targeted reconsideration amendments to 40 CFR parts 60 and 63 covering LDAR modification criteria and monitoring, thermal oxidation and flare operating limits, vapor-recovery compliance, cargo-tank vapor tightness, recordkeeping, reporting, and related technical corrections.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of major-source and area-source gasoline distribution facilities
- Gasoline cargo-tank fleet operators and carriers
- State, local, and Tribal authorities, workers, and nearby communities

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would reduce or clarify selected compliance burdens while retaining the 2024 numerical emission standards, including a possible return toward historical graduated cargo-tank vapor-tightness limits and co-proposed thermal-oxidation compliance options.

**Main Requirements**
- Clarify LDAR surveys, leak repairs, backup-system monitoring, and NSPS modification criteria.
- Offer thermal-oxidation options, set air-assist limits, and revise NHV sampling and records.
- Revise vapor-recovery averaging, cargo-tank certification, and transition requirements.

**Inference:** This is a compliance-architecture reconsideration: make the 2024 standards more practicable without reopening their underlying emission-control determinations.
