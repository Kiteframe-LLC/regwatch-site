## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** Observable Level 2 inputs provide an independent basis for pricing eligible cross trades.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Observable inputs remain sufficiently reliable and comparable for the particular security, trade timing, size, and fund pair.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A price source may use stale, thin, or model-dependent inputs during stressed conditions, while the adviser still treats the source as reasonably representative.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for source-quality standards, stale-price controls, and evidence that Level 2 classifications correlate with fair cross-trade execution.
- **Claim:** NAV pricing can be a cost-effective and protective pricing method.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The next NAV computation uses valuation processes that are timely and sufficiently consistent for both sides of the transaction.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Funds with different valuation processes cannot use NAV pricing, while same-process funds may still face intraday market movement before the next NAV.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The agency should explain how timing differences and shared-process requirements prevent value transfer.
- **Claim:** CCO quarterly and annual reviews provide effective oversight after responsibility moves from the board.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** CCOs have the independence, data access, staffing, and authority to identify patterns that an adviser or portfolio manager may not surface.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A CCO receives incomplete adviser records or relies on a sample that misses repeated small disadvantages, especially where subsequent sales are difficult to match.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The record should specify minimum review inputs, testing expectations, escalation, and board response when a pattern is found.
- **Claim:** The quantified benefits are a lower-bound estimate of savings from restored cross trading.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The selected survey and proxy volumes reasonably represent opportunities that would otherwise have occurred and were lost after rule 2a-5.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Some observed pre-rule volume would not have occurred under current market conditions, or new eligibility and pricing conditions change actual take-up materially.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can request sensitivity analysis using alternative volumes, spreads, market regimes, and take-up rates.
