  **Failure Scenario:** A monitor excluded for missing or invalid data could have captured a materially different local condition, or a valid design value could depend materially on approved missing-day substitutions that are not readily reconstructable by a public reader.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** A useful comment can ask EPA to show the monitor-by-monitor basis and explain why the excluded sites cannot reasonably alter the area-wide conclusion.
- **Claim Id:** claim_invalid_sites_do_not_preclude

  **Failure Scenario:** Local emissions, siting, access, or weather patterns could change the relative ranking of sites across periods, making older five-year comparisons less predictive of the current period.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The final rule should make the temporal and technical basis for using historical comparisons explicit and identify the underlying reports in a way that allows reconstruction.
- **Claim Id:** claim_substitution_validity
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The Regional Administrator's missing-day approvals are supported by meteorological or ambient data showing that the missing days were not conducive to concentrations above the standard.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the substitution analyses or approval letters contain material caveats, a reader relying only on the proposed rule could not assess whether the substituted days were conservative or whether the resulting design values are sensitive to the approval.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can request a concise summary or table of the approved substitutions, their basis, and their effect on the design values.
- **Claim Id:** claim_current_confirmation
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Preliminary January-March 2026 data are sufficiently reliable as a current check even though certification is pending.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Later certification, quality-control review, or seasonal ozone data could change the preliminary indication before final action.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** EPA should state how it will account for new certified or quality-assured data before finalizing and what would cause it to revisit the proposal.
- **Claim Id:** claim_suspension_effect
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Suspending attainment-related planning submissions while retaining monitoring, emissions inventory, NSR, and section 185 requirements is an adequate way to preserve accountability while the area remains nonattainment.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A future deterioration could occur during a period when suspended planning work is no longer being refreshed, leaving stakeholders with less current analysis before a formal redesignation or renewed obligation.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The final action could clarify the continuing monitoring and public-notification pathway and the practical trigger for renewed planning requirements.
