## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** No certification requests since 2020 indicates abandonment.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Certification records capture all commercially relevant use and remaining certified inventory is immaterial.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A firm holds certified product or a market participant has delayed use without seeking a new certification.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** FDA should invite inventory, use, and reliance evidence before finalizing repeal and transition dates.
- **Claim:** A one-year compliance period can allow depletion.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Any remaining supply can be identified and used within one year.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Inventory is distributed across domestic and international supply chains or subject to seasonal orange markets.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The final order should explain the duration using inventory and seasonality evidence.
