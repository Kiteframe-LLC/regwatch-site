## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim Id:** claim_redesignation
  
  **Claim:** The area should be redesignated to attainment.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The current design values remain at or below the standard once the exceptional-event days are excluded.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the exceptional-event record is incomplete or the remaining design values still exceed 0.070 ppm, redesignation would rest on a weak factual base.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can test whether EPA has shown the attainment finding with enough clarity and record support.
- **Claim Id:** claim_maintenance_plan
  
  **Claim:** The updated maintenance plan should be approved through 2040.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Projected emissions, controls, and contingency measures are sufficient to keep the area in attainment for the full maintenance period.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If growth, mobile-source activity, or enforcement assumptions change, the plan may not preserve attainment through 2040.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can ask for the planning assumptions behind the 2040 horizon and contingency design.
- **Claim Id:** claim_budgets
  
  **Claim:** The 2035 and 2040 motor vehicle emissions budgets are approvable.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The budgets are consistent with the maintenance demonstration and transportation conformity requirements.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the budgets are not transparently derived or the safety margin is misallocated, conformity users may be left without a stable record.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can request the budget derivation and safety-margin allocation in a more legible form.
- **Claim Id:** claim_deadline_reset
  
  **Claim:** The Moderate-classification deadlines should be reset to December 5, 2026.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The Sixth Circuit vacatur and Michigan's prior reliance justify a one-time deadline reset.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the deadline reset is too open-ended or not tied to implementation milestones, the accommodation could delay compliance without enough explanation.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can press EPA to explain why these dates, rather than phased milestones, are the right remedy.
- **Claim Id:** claim_basic_im
  
  **Claim:** Basic I/M can reasonably wait until December 5, 2030.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The program has enough build-out complexity that a four-year implementation window is justified.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the record does not show the milestones needed to stand up Basic I/M, the 2030 date may read as a convenience date rather than a necessity.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can ask for the stepwise implementation plan that makes 2030 reasonable.
- **Claim Id:** claim_exceptional_events
  
  **Claim:** The wildfire days should be excluded from the attainment calculation.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The June 29-30, 2023, and July 15, 2025 exceedances satisfy the exceptional-events criteria and are not representative of normal ozone conditions.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the wildfire nexus or the regulatory-significance showing is incomplete, the attainment bridge becomes vulnerable.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments can ask EPA to show the event-to-monitor pathway and the design-value effect more explicitly.
