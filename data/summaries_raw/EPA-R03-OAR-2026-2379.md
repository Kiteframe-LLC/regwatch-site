## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**One Sentence:** EPA proposes to approve Virginia’s Revision D22 into the SIP, making specified Virginia permitting, authority-transfer, and public-participation provisions federally enforceable.

**Proposed Changes**
- Transfer specified permit, order, and variance decisions from the Board to VADEQ.
- Revise procedures for controversial permits, public comments, hearings, and permit application decisions.
- Add 9VAC5-80-1255 and 9VAC5-80-1773 to the SIP, remove obsolete 9VAC5-80-1040, and incorporate selected provisions across Chapters 10, 80, 85, and 170.

**Practical Effect:** Future permitting decisions would operate under the revised VADEQ-centered procedures, with the included provisions enforceable as part of Virginia’s SIP.

**Affected Entities**
- VADEQ and the Virginia State Air Pollution Control Board
- Applicants and operators subject to Virginia operating, minor NSR, PSD, and NNSR permits
- Members of the public and communities participating in controversial-permit hearings and comments
- EPA, through SIP oversight and federal enforceability
