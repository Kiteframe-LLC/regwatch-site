## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** The Department proposes to revise Forest Service directive procedures, narrow the scope of notice and comment under 36 CFR part 216, and reclassify Forest Service Handbooks as advisory and informational guidance.

**Affected Entities**
- Forest Service employees and responsible officials
- members of the public who comment on directive changes
- tribal governments with consultation interests
- small entities that may be affected by handbook-linked permits, fees, or procedures
- commenters who rely on Federal Register or mailed notice practices

**Statutory Authority**
- 5 U.S.C. 301
- 16 U.S.C. 1612(a)

**Key Regulatory Change:** The rule would treat rescissions and advisory guidance as outside the statutory notice-and-comment scope and would allow web-based notice without requiring a physical mailing address.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the Forest Service Manual will be the primary source of mandatory administrative direction.
- The notice says handbook guidance may be deviated from without documentation.
- The notice says additional notices are allowed but not required.

**Inference:** The proposal is a legal and procedural reclassification of directive material, not a technical or data-heavy rulemaking.

**Evidence Quotes**
- primary source of administrative direction
- advisory and informational guidance
- broadly accessible public forum
