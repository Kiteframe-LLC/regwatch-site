## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim Id:** claim_1
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Stock ownership, shareholder status, CFC status, and the relevant ownership period can be identified and converted into a consistent pro rata allocation.

  **Procedural Implication:** Request objective documentation standards, examples, and relief for reasonable reliance on prior guidance.

## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Observed Fact:** The NPRM would replace the former last-day/hypothetical-distribution allocation approach for specified post-OBBBA periods with ownership-and-time-based rules, add rules for section 951B, tested income and tested loss, special stock classes, year closing, transition dividends, and expanded Form 5471 stock reporting.

**Inference:** The practical effect is to shift more allocation and recordkeeping work toward tracking ownership periods, stock classes, distributions, and indirect ownership throughout the year.

**Regulatory Action:** Amend Treasury regulations under sections 951, 951A, 245A, 6038, and 1502 and establish applicability and transition rules.

**Affected Entities**
- U.S. shareholders of foreign corporations
- controlled foreign corporations and foreign controlled foreign corporations
- domestic corporations and consolidated groups
- tax professionals and Form 5471 preparers
- owners of multiple classes of CFC stock

**Statutory Authority:** Sections 951(a)(4), 70354(c)(2), and 7805(a) of the Internal Revenue Code and OBBBA, as described in the notice.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Implement time-based pro rata shares under amended section 951(a)(2), including corresponding rules for tested income and tested loss and related reporting.
