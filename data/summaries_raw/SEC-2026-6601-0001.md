## Step 9 — Failure Modes

- **Failure Mode:** Stale screening basis
  
  **Mechanism:** A rule is placed on the review list based mainly on its 2017 adoption date and a possible impact, without current evidence that the section 610 threshold remains satisfied.
  
  **Possible Effect:** The review may spend limited agency and commenter resources on a rule that is no longer materially burdensome, or overlook a changed burden that is material for only a subset of small entities.
  
  **Mitigation For Record:** Request current entity, filing-volume, burden, and impact data and explain the inclusion screen.
- **Failure Mode:** Under-recorded workflow burden
  
  **Mechanism:** The short rule description treats hyperlinks and HTML as simple obligations without identifying conversion, validation, accessibility, remediation, or correction work.
  
  **Possible Effect:** Comments may understate the real burden or misattribute it to the wrong requirement, weakening the later alternatives analysis.
  
  **Mitigation For Record:** Collect end-to-end workflow accounts from small filers, vendors, and filing reviewers.
- **Failure Mode:** Non-comparable comment record
  
  **Mechanism:** The notice invites broad comments but does not request a common set of measures or identify how the SEC will weigh anecdote, cost data, and public-user evidence.
  
  **Possible Effect:** The record could contain persuasive but incompatible submissions that do not support a transparent choice among continuation, amendment, and rescission.
  
  **Mitigation For Record:** Publish or use a rule-specific evidence checklist covering costs, benefits, errors, affected entities, alternatives, and changed conditions.
- **Failure Mode:** Deadline uncertainty
  
  **Mechanism:** The body and metadata show different comment-end representations.
  
  **Possible Effect:** A commenter could misunderstand the filing cutoff, especially if relying on a docket interface rather than the Federal Register text.
  
  **Mitigation For Record:** Reconcile the date and display one unambiguous deadline with timezone context.
