## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim Id:** claim_340b_price
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The 2026 drug acquisition survey captures acquisition costs well enough to support a precise 2027 payment discount for 340B acquired drugs.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If acquisition patterns vary by hospital type, timing, or product mix, a single discount may understate or overstate actual acquisition cost relationships.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to publish the survey design, weighting, and sensitivity analysis that connect the survey to the proposed ASP-minus formula.
- **Claim Id:** claim_340b_offset
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The move from a 0.5 percent to a 3 percent annual reduction properly tracks the remaining amount needed to offset the earlier 340B remedy payments.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the prior offset already over- or under-recovers the earlier payment changes, the 2027 adjustment could shift costs more than the record supports.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to show the bridge from the 2018-2022 remedy to the new reduction rate and to present a sensitivity analysis.
- **Claim Id:** claim_prior_auth_botox
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Adding botulinum toxin injection services to prior authorization will reduce unnecessary use without materially slowing medically appropriate care.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If approvals, denials, or appeal handling are slow or inconsistent, patients and clinicians may face added delay even when care is clinically appropriate.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS for turnaround-time expectations, exceptions, appeal pathways, and access-impact metrics.
- **Claim Id:** claim_pbd_imaging
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Applying the Physician Fee Schedule equivalent rate to imaging without contrast APCs will curb unnecessary volume growth without creating unintended access distortions.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If coding or site-of-service distinctions are imperfect, the policy could shift cases in ways that do not match clinical necessity or local capacity.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to show the volume data and site-of-service effects that justify the off-campus PBD adjustment and the rural Sole Community Hospital exemption.
- **Claim Id:** claim_price_transparency
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Requesting input on complex contracting terms will lead to a more comparable and useful Hospital Price Transparency record.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If contract mechanisms remain defined inconsistently, hospitals may continue to disclose information that is technically posted but not meaningfully comparable.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to standardize field definitions, validation checks, and correction pathways for machine-readable files and consumer-friendly displays.
- **Claim Id:** claim_qrp_changes
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Removing one cross-program measure and refining validation procedures will preserve useful accountability while reducing unnecessary reporting burden.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If no replacement signal or stratification is available, comparability may worsen even as burden falls.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS what measurement continuity, stratification, or replacement strategy will preserve the quality signal after the measure removal.
