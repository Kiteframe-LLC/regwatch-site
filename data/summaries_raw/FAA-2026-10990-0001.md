## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** Remove non-insulin hypoglycemic drugs from the specifically disqualifying diabetes language for first-, second-, and third-class certificates, allowing qualified NIDDM applicants to be evaluated by AMEs under general standards rather than automatically deferred for Special Issuance.

**Affected Entities**
- NIDDM applicants and certificate holders
- Aviation Medical Examiners
- FAA medical review staff
- Air carriers, operators, flight schools, and other employers
- Certain non-pilot second-class certificate holders

**Core Tradeoff:** The proposal reduces delay and FAA workload while transferring more screening responsibility to AMEs and relying on general standards to preserve safety.

**Proposal Stage Note:** As an NPRM, the record supports constructive requests for evidence, safeguards, and monitoring.
