## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** market_basket_rebase
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The 2024 cost structure and supporting proxies adequately capture current ESRD facility input costs.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the cost mix shifted after the base year or the proxies miss dialysis-specific changes, the recalibrated market basket could understate or overstate real costs.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to show the sensitivity of the rebased market basket to alternative cost weights and updated utilization data.
- **Claim:** base_rate_binder
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The $15.96 add-on and TDAPA transition-end treatment reflect the true operational cost of phosphate binders.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If utilization, acquisition cost, or operational cost assumptions differ across facilities, the bundled amount could miscalibrate payment.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for the cost model and utilization assumptions used to derive the binder incorporation amount.
- **Claim:** lvpa_threshold
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** An 8,000-treatment cutoff and 6-tier structure target low-volume facilities without creating a sharp cliff.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Facilities near the threshold may experience abrupt payment changes that do not match incremental cost differences.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to publish the facility-size distribution around the threshold and the expected payment effects by tier.
- **Claim:** pediatric_adjusters
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Permanent pediatric adjusters can replace the temporary TPEAPA without leaving a coverage gap or overcorrecting costs.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If pediatric-specific cost data remain thin, the permanent policy could be too high, too low, or uneven across modalities.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to identify the pediatric cost evidence and transition analysis supporting the permanent adjusters.
- **Claim:** qip_updates
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The Hyperphosphatemia and NHSN BSI updates measure meaningful quality differences rather than administrative proxies.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the measures capture process compliance more than patient outcomes, facilities may face burden without a commensurate quality signal.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask CMS to explain the validity evidence and expected burden changes for each measure update.

  **Description:** The 8,000-treatment LVPA cutoff and tier structure may create abrupt payment changes for facilities near the boundary.
