- **Claim:** Access and amendment rights could compromise insider-risk investigations.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Subjects will receive information whose disclosure would reveal investigative interests, evidence, or confidential sources, and no narrower protection would adequately address that risk.

## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** The proposal would add CFTC-59 to 17 CFR 146.12(h) and exempt it from specified Privacy Act provisions on disclosure accounting, access, amendment, relevancy, agency requirements, and agency rules.

**Affected Entities:** Individuals whose information is in CFTC-59, CFTC staff and contractors, the Commission, and confidential sources.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice invokes 5 U.S.C. 552a(k)(2), the Privacy Act, the APA, and Part 146 authority.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The exemption would reduce access, amendment, and related transparency for CFTC-59 records.
