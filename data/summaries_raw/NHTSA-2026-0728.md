## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** Modernize FMVSS No. 135 by exempting ADS-only vehicles from manual brake-control requirements, adapting definitions and test inputs, revising telltale rules, and removing obsolete ABS-related provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- ADS vehicle manufacturers and developers
- vehicle certifying entities and NHTSA compliance testers
- rideshare operators
- passengers in ADS-only vehicles
- manufacturers of conventional light vehicles

**Core Tradeoff:** Design flexibility and reduced legacy-control burden versus independent testability, clear occupant warnings, and accountability for ADS braking behavior.
