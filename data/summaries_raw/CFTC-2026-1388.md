## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** RFI asking whether and under what conditions the CFTC should permit or evaluate 24/7 trading of standard energy futures and perpetual contracts referencing physically delivered or storable energy commodities.

**Affected Entities:** Designated contract markets, derivatives clearing organizations, clearing members, energy producers, refiners, merchants, transporters, end users, institutional investors, retail traders, and commercial firms that rely on energy futures benchmarks in physical or financing contracts.

**Statutory Authority:** Commodity Exchange Act core principles for designated contract markets, including anti-manipulation, surveillance, and position-limit requirements discussed through Core Principles 3, 4, and 5 and Appendix C to part 38.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No rule text is proposed. The document instead asks how the Commission should think about reference-price reliability, manipulation resistance, settlement infrastructure, position limits, funding mechanics, customer protection, and market effects before allowing these product structures to expand in energy markets.
