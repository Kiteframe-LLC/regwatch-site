## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** Objective criteria can clarify innovative-product classifications.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Product features can be mapped consistently to statutory prongs and exclusions.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** The same feature produces different classifications depending on market structure or use, leaving participants with the same uncertainty.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments should provide concrete products and testable decision rules.
- **Claim:** Alternative compliance may work for substantially similar requirements.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The Commissions can compare scope, objectives, outcomes, supervision, reporting, and enforcement without weakening either regime.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A broad similarity test could create gaps in information sharing or permit one framework to substitute for a materially different safeguard.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments should identify requirements that can and cannot be substituted and explain why.
- **Claim:** Cross-market surveillance can preserve market integrity under coordination.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The agencies will have reliable shared data, examination access, and enforcement responsibility.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Uneven authority or monitoring capacity creates a jurisdictional gap before a violation is detected.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comments should specify minimum surveillance and escalation safeguards.
