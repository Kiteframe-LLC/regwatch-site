## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** Existing racemic-glufosinate metabolism and residue information can support L-glufosinate-P-ammonium tolerances.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The relevant residue definition, metabolites, kinetics, and analytical recovery are sufficiently comparable across the isomeric and racemic materials.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If stereochemistry changes absorption, metabolism, residue distribution, or analytical response in a commodity or livestock matrix, the bridge could understate residues or uncertainty.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Ask EPA to identify the comparison criteria and explain why the existing residue definition remains protective for every requested commodity.
- **Claim:** The petition's dietary exposure estimates show no concern.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The 2024 assessment's scaled concentrations, field-trial data, drinking-water estimates, and 100-percent-crop-treated assumption adequately represent the proposed uses.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** A different use pattern, residue distribution, drinking-water scenario, or treatment fraction could change the highest exposed subgroup or cumulative assessment.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Request the input tables, scaling rationale, sensitivity cases, and subgroup results underlying the quoted percentages.
- **Claim:** The proposed tolerance list can account for related commodities and indirect residues.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The listed commodities and livestock feed pathways capture foreseeable transfer and enforcement needs.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Omitted feed, animal, processed-food, or rotational-crop pathways could create gaps between authorized use and measurable residues.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Comment with commodity-specific pathways and ask EPA to reconcile the petition list with its Food and Feed Commodity Vocabulary.
- **Claim:** The filing notice provides enough information for useful public comment.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Petition summaries and referenced assessments are accessible and sufficiently detailed to allow independent technical review.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the decisive risk assessment, residue trials, or analytical validation data are not publicly available, commenters cannot test the proposed levels before EPA acts.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Ask EPA to docket or identify the decisive materials and extend or reopen comment if material data become available late.
