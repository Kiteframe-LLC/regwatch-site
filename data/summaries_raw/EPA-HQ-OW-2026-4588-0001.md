## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Observed Facts**
- EPA proposes to approve Alaska's application for Class VI UIC primacy under SDWA sections 1422 and 1450 and to amend 40 CFR part 147.
- The proposed transfer covers Class VI wells in Alaska outside Indian country and areas of exclusive Federal jurisdiction; EPA would continue direct administration for the excluded areas and for Class I, III, IV, and V wells.
- Approval would incorporate specified Alaska statutes and regulations by reference and recognize the program description, MOA, Governor's letter, and Attorney General statement as program elements.
- Class VI permits would address site characterization, monitoring, operating limits, financial assurance, emergency response, reporting, and post-injection obligations.

**Inference:** The proposal is primarily a governance and implementation transfer: it does not lower the stated Federal Class VI protection baseline, but it changes which public agency performs permitting, compliance evaluation, and enforcement for covered wells.

**Regulatory Action:** Approve Alaska's Class VI UIC primacy application and incorporate the approved State program into 40 CFR part 147, subject to geographic exclusions and continuing EPA oversight.

**Affected Entities**
- Alaska Oil and Gas Conservation Commission
- EPA Region 10 and EPA program offices
- owners and operators seeking Class VI permits
- surface and subsurface landowners and USDW-dependent communities
- Tribal governments and technical contractors supporting State review

**Statutory Authority:** Safe Drinking Water Act sections 1421, 1422, and 1450; 40 CFR parts 124, 144, 145, 146, and 147.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Covered Class VI permitting and enforcement would move to AOGCC, with EPA oversight and direct authority in excluded areas and for other well classes.

**Evidence**
- **Section:** Summary and V.A Proposed Action
  
  **Quote:** The EPA's approval of the State's UIC program primacy application would allow the Alaska Oil and Gas Conservation Commission (AOGCC) to issue and enforce compliance with UIC Class VI permits.
