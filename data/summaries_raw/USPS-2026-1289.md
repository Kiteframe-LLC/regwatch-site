  **Failure Scenario:** Incomplete portal submissions, local-list changes, replacement ballots, late court orders, emergency ballots, or vendor mistakes produce mismatches that delay or reject outbound ballots.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for fallback processes, error correction windows, audit logs, emergency procedures, and service standards for USPS review.
- **Claim Id:** claim_law_enforcement

  **Procedural Implication:** USPS should provide evidence on scan reliability, false-positive risks, and how data will or will not be used by law-enforcement entities.

  **Failure Scenario:** USPS refusal to accept nonmatching outbound ballot mail effectively creates a federal postal gateway that can affect whether state-authorized ballots are sent on time.

## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** USPS proposes new DMM section 705.24 for federal ballot mail, requiring official Election Mail logos, automation compatibility, uniquely serialized Intelligent Mail barcodes for outbound and return ballot envelopes, mailpiece design review, a Federal Ballot Mail Portal, state-specific voter-and-barcode participation lists, certification duties, and USPS pre-acceptance verification for outbound federal ballot mail.

**Affected Entities**
- State chief election officials
- Local election officials and political subdivisions that mail ballots
- Mail service providers authorized by election officials
- Voters who receive or return mail-in or absentee ballots in federal elections
- USPS acceptance, processing, technical-assistance, and election-mail operations personnel
- Election officials responsible for privacy, data security, ballot timing, and replacement-ballot workflows

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal excludes primary elections and UOCAVA ballots.
- The optional 90-day pre-mailing notice does not block later enrollment.
- Ballot Portal Users must provide names, addresses, outbound IMbs, return IMbs, and originating election office state.
- USPS would return noncompliant outbound federal ballot mail to the authorized ballot mailer before acceptance.
- USPS says the proposal does not alter voting eligibility and that states remain responsible for the contents of participation lists.

**Inference**
- The rule is not merely a packaging standard; it creates a new list-based operational control point for federal ballot mail.
- High-value public comments should focus on statutory fit, privacy design, portal readiness, fallback procedures, cost burden, scan-data reliability, and effects on state election deadlines.

**Evidence Quotes**
- Mailings that do not comply with 24.5.1 and 24.5.2 will not be accepted and will be returned to the authorized ballot mailer.
- The Postal Service will also take steps necessary for the creation of a new system of records notice.
- The Postal Service does not purport to alter the eligibility of any individual to vote under state or federal law.
