## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** Treasury and IRS would establish rules for employer contributions to Trump Accounts, including separate written-plan, eligibility, notice, certification, trustee communication, reporting, correction, and nondiscrimination requirements, while updating section 129 dependent-care nondiscrimination rules.

**Mechanism:** Tax exclusion is conditioned on plan and operational compliance. Failure of nondiscrimination rules generally removes exclusion for HCEs while preserving it for NHCEs, subject to proposed correction mechanisms.

**Affected Entities:** Employers, employees and dependents using Trump Accounts, trustees and payroll/service providers, highly compensated employees, non-highly compensated employees, principal owners, and dependent-care plan administrators.

**Central Tradeoff:** The proposal seeks administrable safeguards against discriminatory tax benefits, but adds verification, reporting, testing, and correction burdens whose costs and operational feasibility are not quantified in the available record.

**Observed Fact:** The proposal includes a $2,500 annual per-employee exclusion limit for section 128 contributions, a $7,500 dependent-care exclusion limit ($3,750 for married filing separately), and a 55 percent average-benefits threshold.

**Inference:** The practical effect will depend on trustee data interfaces, payroll implementation, plan demographics, and how employers handle failed tests or corrected contributions.
