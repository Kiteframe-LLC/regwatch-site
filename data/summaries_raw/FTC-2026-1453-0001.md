## Step 9 — Failure Modes

- **Mode:** Scope sprawl
  
  **Mechanism:** A cross-sector definition of material consumer decision could cover materially different workflows.
  
  **Consequence:** Unclear obligations and comments that cannot be compared across sectors.
  
  **Mitigation Question:** Can the Commission define covered decisions by harm, sector, or decision function?
- **Mode:** Confidentiality exception swallowing the rule
  
  **Mechanism:** Trade-secret, security, privacy, or law-enforcement limits may leave only a generic explanation.
  
  **Consequence:** The formal right exists but does not permit meaningful correction or review.
  
  **Mitigation Question:** What non-sensitive minimum explanation, record, and reviewer information can always be provided?
- **Mode:** Human-review formalism
  
  **Mechanism:** A nominal reviewer lacks authority, information, independence, or time to correct the result.
  
  **Consequence:** Review becomes a procedural label rather than a corrective safeguard.
  
  **Mitigation Question:** What evidence would show that review can change outcomes and address the stated reason?
- **Mode:** Compliance burden without proportionality
  
  **Mechanism:** Uniform record and explanation duties apply to small entities or low-risk decisions.
  
  **Consequence:** Costs and operational friction are imposed without corresponding consumer benefit.
  
  **Mitigation Question:** What tiering, thresholds, or safe harbors are appropriate?
