## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

### Claim A

**Claim:** EPA has received a petition that contains the data or information prescribed by FFDCA section 408(d)(2) and 40 CFR 180.7(b).

**Underlying assumption:** The completeness screen is adequate to start public comment even though EPA has not completed a merits review.

**Failure scenario:** If important supporting documents are not publicly accessible or are too summarized for independent review, commenters may have difficulty evaluating the petition before the comment deadline.

**Procedural implication:** Commenters can ask EPA to identify and place in the docket the residue chemistry review, JMPR materials relied on, and any EPA risk assessments that will materially support a final tolerance decision.

### Claim B

**Claim:** The petition requests a 15 ppm tolerance for mancozeb residues on imported longan.

**Underlying assumption:** A tolerance at the Codex-aligned level is appropriate for U.S. enforcement and consumer exposure review.

**Failure scenario:** If residue trials, import patterns, processing assumptions, or U.S. consumption data differ from the conditions underlying the Codex maximum residue limit, the requested level may need adjustment or additional explanation.

**Procedural implication:** Commenters can ask EPA to explain how the Codex/JMPR residue basis maps onto U.S. dietary exposure assumptions and FFDCA safety standards.

### Claim C

**Claim:** Mancozeb is measured for enforcement by degradation to CS2 using the Keppel colorimetric method.

**Underlying assumption:** The method is sufficiently sensitive, specific, and practical for longan imports at the proposed tolerance level.

**Failure scenario:** If the method measures dithiocarbamates as a group or has commodity-specific interference, enforcement clarity could suffer.

**Procedural implication:** Commenters can ask EPA to explain method validation for longan and how the enforcement method distinguishes or attributes residues where multiple dithiocarbamate sources may be relevant.

### Claim D

**Claim:** The petitioner asserts reasonable certainty of no harm from aggregate exposure to mancozeb residues and ETU from imported longan.

**Underlying assumption:** The cited 2023 EPA risk assessments and ETU exposure estimates remain current and adequately account for infants, children, high-end consumers, and cumulative ETU exposure from EBDC fungicides.

**Failure scenario:** If current exposure estimates, drinking-water assumptions, import volumes, or toxicology inputs have changed, EPA may need updated analysis before finalizing the tolerance.

**Procedural implication:** Commenters can request a transparent EPA explanation of how imported longan affects aggregate mancozeb/ETU exposure and how EPA applies the FQPA safety factor.
