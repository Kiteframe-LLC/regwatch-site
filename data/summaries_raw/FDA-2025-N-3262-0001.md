## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** FDA proposes to require GRAS notices for covered human- and animal-food uses, revise GRAS definitions and procedures, require electronic and English submissions, address disclosure and noncompliance, provide a limited transition pathway, and revise related threshold-of-regulation and animal-food provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- food and ingredient manufacturers
- chemical and food-contact-substance firms
- animal-food businesses
- foreign notifiers
- small entities
- FDA reviewers and the public

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal applies to substances introduced into interstate commerce under the GRAS provision, subject to listed exceptions.
- The notice requests comment on alternatives, effective/compliance dates, costs, benefits, and the PRA collection.

**Inference**
- The main policy leverage point is whether mandatory transparency is designed with sufficiently clear boundaries, transition rules, review practices, and burden estimates.

**Evidence Quotes**
- require the submission of generally recognized as safe (GRAS) notices
- greater transparency about substances that are added to food
