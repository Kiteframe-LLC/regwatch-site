## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim:** The 2023-2025 monitoring record is enough to support redesignation.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The exceptional-event exclusions and representative-monitor logic are valid and stable.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the excluded wildfire days are not accepted, the Muskegon monitor remains at or above the standard and the attainment premise narrows.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for a compact explanation of the exclusion logic and its sensitivity to the five disputed days.
- **Claim:** Additional serious NOx RACT would not materially improve ozone in West Michigan.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Transport from upwind states dominates local NOx contributions at the shoreline monitors.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If local NOx reductions have more effect than the addendum suggests, the waiver rationale becomes narrower.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The record would benefit from a simple local-versus-transport comparison that a public reader can test.
- **Claim:** No non-CTG VOC major source remains in Muskegon.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The 10 tpy proxy, 2.7 tpy unit screen, permit review, and field checks exhaust the relevant candidate sources.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If a borderline source or permit condition was missed, Muskegon could require case-by-case VOC RACT.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for a source-by-source disposition table and any borderline cases that were screened out.
- **Claim:** The Berrien landfills already meet serious VOC RACT.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The existing landfill-gas controls and permit conditions are the least unreasonable controls available.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If enclosed flares, biofilters, or biocovers are reasonably cost-effective, the chosen RACT endpoint needs more explanation.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** The agency should show the control-cost comparison in a public-facing way.
- **Claim:** The 2032 and 2036 MVEBs are adequate for conformity.
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The budgets are derived consistently from the maintenance plan and the projected onroad inventory.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** If the derivation is opaque or the safety margin is overstated, the budgets are harder to audit.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for the budget derivation and the safety-margin arithmetic.

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to redesignate the Berrien and Muskegon areas to attainment, approve the maintenance plans and conformity budgets, approve the enhanced monitoring plan, approve serious VOC RACT for the remaining non-CTG sources, and approve a section 182(f) NOx waiver.

## Step 5 — Missing Alternatives Detector

**Regulatory Objective:** Approve the Muskegon/Berrien serious ozone package while keeping the administrative record defensible for public comment.

**Alternatives Evaluated**
- Continue serious nonattainment treatment until the remaining elements are separately approved.
- Use the NOx waiver and existing transport record instead of additional serious NOx RACT.
- Use the Muskegon negative declaration and case-by-case VOC RACT screening for remaining non-CTG sources.
- Use existing landfill-gas controls and permit conditions as the serious VOC RACT endpoint for Berrien.

**Plausible Alternatives Not Analyzed**
- Publish a single source-disposition table for the Muskegon screening chain.
- Provide a compact before-and-after derivation of the 2032 and 2036 conformity budgets and safety margin.
- Show a side-by-side cost and emission comparison for the Berrien landfill retrofit alternatives.
- Test the 10 tpy proxy and 2.7 tpy unit screen against a small sensitivity band to show whether any borderline sources remain.

**Evidence Suggesting Feasibility**
- The addendum already relies on emissions screening, permit review, and field verification, so a compact disposition table would be an extension of the current method rather than a new theory.
- The notice and attachments already discuss the control systems, budget numbers, and waiver criteria, which means a more compact public crosswalk appears feasible without changing the substance.

**Comment Angle:** Ask EPA to publish a single public crosswalk and a short sensitivity appendix for the decisive screens.
