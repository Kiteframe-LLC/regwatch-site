## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Observed Facts:** The NPRM would require earlier and more frequent Exchange verification, pause APTC and CSR payment for specified unresolved DMIs, require annual confirmation for subsequent-year assistance, and create pended-plan and first-of-month effective-date rules. It cites ACA sections 1311, 1401, 1402, and 1411, WFTC section 71303, and the existing part 155 authority citation.

**Regulatory Action:** The affected entities are Marketplace applicants and enrollees, Exchanges, QHP issuers, agents, brokers, assisters, State Medicaid and CHIP agencies, and tax filers.

**Inference:** The proposal shifts eligibility administration toward verification as a gateway to affordability and coverage, while using pended selection and exceptions as mitigations.

**Core Tradeoff:** Stronger program integrity may reduce improper subsidy payment, but delayed or erroneous verification can create affordability, continuity, administrative, and federalism costs.

## Step 5 — Missing Alternatives Detector

  **Alternatives Evaluated**
  - Maintain higher previously verified APTC during the inconsistency period

## Step 9 — Failure Modes

  **Trigger:** Data mismatch, unavailable source, or documentation review delay

  **Detectability:** Should be measured through DMI resolution time, reversal, appeal, and coverage-churn metrics
- **Failure Mode:** Missed annual confirmation window
  
  **Trigger:** Notice failure, unstable contact information, language/access barrier, or insufficient assister capacity
  
  **Consequence:** Otherwise eligible enrollee loses APTC or CSR for the next plan year
  
  **Detectability:** Track completion and subsidy-loss rates by consumer and Exchange characteristics
- **Failure Mode:** Cross-system effective-date mismatch
  
  **Trigger:** Exchange, issuer, or binder-payment system handles a 15th-of-month case inconsistently
  
  **Consequence:** Coverage start, retroactive premium, or claims-processing dispute
  
  **Detectability:** Requires transaction-level reconciliation and consumer complaint monitoring
- **Failure Mode:** State Exchange readiness shortfall
  
  **Trigger:** System build, testing, procurement, or notice work is incomplete by PY 2028
  
  **Consequence:** Uneven implementation or delayed enrollment
  
  **Detectability:** Publish readiness milestones and contingency status before implementation
