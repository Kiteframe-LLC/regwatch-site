## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve remaining Tennessee SO2 nonattainment SIP elements, approve a CAA section 175A maintenance plan, determine that Sullivan County has attained the 2010 one-hour SO2 NAAQS, and redesignate the area to attainment.

**Legal Effect:** The action would approve and incorporate specified state permit conditions and controls into the Tennessee SIP and change the area designation in 40 CFR part 81.

**Mechanisms**
- Natural-gas repowering of B-253 boilers
- Dry sorbent injection at B-83 boilers 23 and 24
- Combined and source-specific SO2 limits
- Monitoring, recordkeeping, reporting, and permit enforcement
- Maintenance-plan contingency measures

**Affected Entities:** Sullivan County residents and communities; Eastman Chemical and Primester; Tennessee air-quality authorities; nearby modeled sources; and commenters using the SIP record.

**Central Rationale:** Historical SO2 reductions, attaining monitoring data, and a modeled result below the NAAQS are attributed to enforceable controls that EPA proposes to preserve through redesignation.

**Unresolved Questions**
- How sensitive is 74.82 ppb to model and emissions assumptions?
- Why is the excluded batch source immaterial for one-hour SO2?
- How will superseding permit conditions be reconciled?
- Can maintenance respond before four exceedances?

**Stage:** Proposal stage.
