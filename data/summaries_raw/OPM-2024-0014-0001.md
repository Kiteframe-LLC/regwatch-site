## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal adds special retirement-provision subparts for CSRS and FERS employees who suffer qualifying duty-related illness or injury on or after December 9, 2024.
- It preserves enhanced retirement deductions, contributions, accrual, and annuity treatment in a deemed covered position, while excluding pay treatment and mandatory separation from that deemed status.
- It provides certification, notice, waiver, loss-of-coverage, retirement-computation, and appeal provisions.

**Inference:** The rule converts a statutory protection into an administrative eligibility and benefits-preservation system whose practical fairness depends on agency certification, placement searches, notice, and recordkeeping.

**Affected Entities**
- covered Federal first responders
- employing and reappointing Federal agencies
- OPM
- agency medical and human-resources officials
- MSPB
- Federal retirement systems

**Statutory Authority:** 5 U.S.C. 8336(c), 8412(d), 8347(a), 8461(g), 8151, and section 3 of Public Law 117-225.

**Key Regulatory Change:** New 5 CFR subparts 831.M and 842.M plus conforming workers' compensation and pay-retention provisions.
