## Step 1 — Claim Stress Test

- **Claim Id:** claim_threshold_update
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** A $30 billion line better matches the risk and reporting profile of banks that should still use the small-institution methodology.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Institutions just below and above $30 billion remain economically similar, so the new line merely shifts assessments and reporting without improving risk differentiation.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask the FDIC to show why $30 billion is superior to nearby thresholds and whether the reclassification cliff is justified.
- **Claim Id:** claim_cpiw_method
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** A four-year CPI-W update cycle preserves the threshold in real terms without creating excessive churn.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Inflation volatility, deflation, or prolonged price changes make the threshold drift materially before the next update.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can request sensitivity analysis on annual, biennial, and four-year adjustments and on how the FDIC would handle unusual inflation paths.
- **Claim Id:** claim_rate_reduction
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The proposed 2 bps and 1 bps reductions lower assessments without weakening risk differentiation or fund adequacy.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** The rate cuts disproportionately shift burden across banks or reduce revenue more than the agency’s analysis anticipates.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask for bank-level distribution tables, not just aggregate effects, and for the assumptions behind the annual assessment estimates.
- **Claim Id:** claim_rra
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** Virtual data room testing and prescribed data access measurably reduce resolution loss and are worth the offered credit.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** The testing exercise becomes a compliance checklist with limited connection to real resolution performance, or the data-access path is too burdensome to use widely.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can request the empirical basis for the 0.5 bps increments and the operational rules that make the credit measurable and secure.
- **Claim Id:** claim_benefits_justify_costs
  
  **Underlying Assumption:** The RIA captures the main cost and benefit channels well enough to support the conclusion.
  
  **Failure Scenario:** Transition costs, vendor costs, and institution-specific compliance burdens are undercounted, so the aggregate net-benefit conclusion is overstated.
  
  **Procedural Implication:** Commenters can ask the FDIC to separate steady-state effects from transition effects and to disclose any omitted compliance or implementation costs.

## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** The FDIC proposes under sections 7 and 9 of the FDI Act to amend 12 CFR part 327 by raising the small/large institution threshold to $30 billion, indexing it every four years to CPI-W, lowering assessment rate schedules, adding a resolution readiness adjustment, and removing obsolete provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- insured depository institutions near the small/large threshold
- large and highly complex institutions subject to the scorecard methodology
- small institutions and insured branches of foreign banks
- institutions considering VDR testing or prescribed data access for the RRA
- commenters and service providers involved in resolution-readiness compliance

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the current threshold has been in place since 2006 and would be adjusted every four years for inflation.
- The notice estimates that about 76 institutions would move from the large-institution method to the small-institution method.
- The notice also says the proposal would lower initial base assessment rate schedules and create an RRA credit tied to testing and data access.

**Inference:** The rule is a recoding of assessment classes and incentives rather than a wholly new assessment system; the strongest comments will focus on where the threshold is drawn, how often it is updated, and how the RRA works in practice.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The FDIC, under its general rulemaking authority in section 9 of the FDI Act, and its specific authority under section 7 of the FDI Act...
- The FDIC is proposing to update the asset-based threshold ... from $10 billion ... to $30 billion.
- The proposed rule would amend the risk-based assessment system to include a downward adjustment, the RRA.
