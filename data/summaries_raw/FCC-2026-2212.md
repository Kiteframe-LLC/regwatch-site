## Step 2 — Essence of the Rule

**Regulatory Action:** Seek comment on reforming legacy and soon-to-sunset high-cost universal-service support, potentially creating a new fixed-support mechanism, extending A-CAM I, and attaching deployment, performance, cybersecurity, reporting, and other conditions.

**Affected Entities**
- rate-of-return carriers
- small telecommunications carriers
- rural households and communities
- unserved and underserved broadband locations
- existing support recipients
- competitors and satellite providers
- USF contributors and federal ratepayers
- states, Tribes, and BEAD administrators

**Core Tradeoff:** The FCC is weighing predictable rural network support and universal-service access against finite-fund efficiency, overbuilding risk, transition costs, and stronger service obligations.

**Why It Matters:** The mechanisms discussed account for approximately $1.6 billion in support, and the choices could change both carrier revenues and broadband obligations in rural areas.
