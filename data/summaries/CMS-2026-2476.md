# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2476-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** CMS proposes to update the indirect hold harmless test for health care-related taxes, replace the pre-October 1, 2026 threshold framework with a new statutory threshold approach, add reporting duties, and revise enforcement and oversight mechanics.

**Affected Entities**
- States that impose health care-related taxes
- State Medicaid agencies and finance staff
- Health care providers subject to taxes
- Health insurers covered by the new permissible class
- CMS reviewers who must calculate and publish thresholds
- Patients and Medicaid beneficiaries if state financing changes affect Medicaid budgets

**Observed Facts**
- The notice sets out a new threshold calculation based on taxes enacted and imposed as of July 4, 2025.
- The notice requires interim and final reporting deadlines and says CMS will apply threshold calculations on a Federal fiscal year basis.
- The notice also states that CMS will deduct revenues from taxes that do not meet the requirements of § 433.68.

**Inference**
- This is primarily a Medicaid financing and compliance rule, not merely a definitional cleanup.
- The main comment leverage is likely to come from data quality, implementation feasibility, and the rationale for removing or redesigning the older indirect hold harmless backstop.
