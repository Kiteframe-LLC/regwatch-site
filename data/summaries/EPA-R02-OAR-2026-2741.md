# Public Summary: EPA-R02-OAR-2026-2741-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve Puerto Rico's negative declaration for existing crude oil and natural gas facilities subject to the greenhouse-gas emission guidelines in 40 CFR part 60, subpart OOOOc, and to amend 40 CFR part 62, subpart BBB to reflect that approval.

**Affected Entities**
- Puerto Rico DNER and EPA Region 2
- existing or potentially qualifying crude oil and natural gas facilities in Puerto Rico
- residents and organizations interested in methane regulation

**Statutory Authority**
- Clean Air Act section 111(d), 42 U.S.C. 7411(d)
- 40 CFR 60.23a(b), 62.06, 60.5362c(b), and part 62, subpart A

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposed amendment would formally record Puerto Rico's approved negative declaration in the federal plan regulations rather than add a Puerto Rico state plan for this source category.

**Observed Facts**
- DNER's March 26, 2025 letter says it reviewed its facility inventory and certifies that no existing facilities meet the subpart OOOOc designated-facility definition.
- EPA proposes approval and codification, and solicits comment on the regulatory interpretations and facility analyses.

**Inference**
- The proposal is an approval and codification action; the main comment leverage is transparency around the negative finding and its continuing scope.

**Evidence Quotes**
- DNER certifies that there are no existing crude oil or natural gas facilities that meet the definition of a Designated Facility under the Subpart OOOOc.
