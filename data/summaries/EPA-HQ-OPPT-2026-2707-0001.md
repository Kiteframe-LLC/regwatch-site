# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPPT-2026-2707-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes significant new use rules under TSCA section 5 for 14 PMN-based chemical substances, with a 90-day SNUN notice requirement before any significant new use may begin.

**Affected Entities**
- manufacturers, including importers
- processors
- exporters subject to TSCA section 12(b)
- small businesses that would submit a SNUN
- commenters trying to reconstruct chemical-specific controls from a largely CBI record

**Statutory Authority**
- TSCA section 5(a)(2)
- TSCA section 5(e)(1)(A)
- TSCA section 5(f)(4)
- 40 CFR part 721

**Key Regulatory Change:** The rule turns the absence of existing TSCA-order restrictions into a public significant-new-use trigger for the broader market, not just the original PMN submitters.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says EPA is proposing SNURs for substances subject to prior TSCA orders.
- The economic analysis says 14 substances are covered by PMN review and 0 by MCAN review.
- The rule body says persons who intend to manufacture or process the substance for a significant new use must notify EPA 90 days in advance.

**Inference:** This is a standard order-follow-on TSCA SNUR package, not a new risk determination by itself.

**Evidence Quotes**
- proposing SNURs for the chemical substances
- notify EPA at least 90 days
- follow a TSCA order with a SNUR
