# Public Summary: EPA-R03-OAR-2026-6205-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve WVDEP's negative declarations and amend 40 CFR part 62 to reflect that West Virginia has no existing LMWC or OSWI subject to the cited section 111(d)/129 requirements. The declarations would serve in lieu of state plans.

**Observed Facts:** The notice cites the CAA and implementing regulations; two July 9, 2026 WVDEP letters state that no covered LMWC or OSWI exists; the notice says the declarations were submitted July 13, 2026 and that the action imposes no additional requirements.

**Inference:** The central comment question is whether the no-source finding is documented, correctly matched to federal applicability criteria, and paired with a clear correction path if conditions change.

**Evidence Quotes:** “there are no existing ... subject to” the cited requirements; “may submit a negative declaration in lieu of a State plan.”

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is legally and administratively coherent, but the decisive no-source finding is not reproducible from the reviewed materials and its safeguard for changed conditions is not visible.
