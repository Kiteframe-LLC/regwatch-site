# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2024-0156-0003

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA is seeking comment on a group of pesticide petitions that would establish, modify, remove, or exempt tolerances for residues on various crops and related commodities.

**Affected Entities**
- Pesticide petitioners and registrants
- Growers and commodity groups for the listed crops
- Food processors and handlers that rely on tolerance decisions
- State, Tribal, and Federal reviewers who will later enforce the tolerance framework
- Public commenters concerned with residue, exposure, and labeling consequences

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with related references to 40 CFR 180.7.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is styled as a notice of filing petitions and request for comment.
- EPA says it has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data yet.
- The notice discusses both tolerance changes and tolerance exemptions across multiple petitions.
- The notice also flags commodity vocabulary, harmonization, and related-commodity issues as things commenters should consider.

**Inference:** The record is about record-building for multiple petition outcomes, not about finalizing a single uniform regulatory change.

**Evidence Quotes**
- Notice of filing of petitions and request for comment
- not fully evaluated the sufficiency
- additional data may be needed
- related commodities
