# Public Summary: OPM-2026-0397-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Amends 5 CFR parts 351, 630, and 715 to codify administrative-leave examples and prohibitions, clarify RIF notice treatment, and clarify resignation-withdrawal discretion.

**Affected Entities**
- Federal agencies
- Federal employees covered by title 5 leave and resignation rules
- HR and payroll officials
- Employees considering deferred resignation, RIF-related leave, or resignation withdrawal

**Statutory Authority**
- 5 U.S.C. 6329a(c)(1)
- 5 U.S.C. 1302
- 5 U.S.C. 3301
- 5 U.S.C. 3302
- 5 U.S.C. 7301
- 5 U.S.C. 3502

**Key Regulatory Changes**
- Replace grant with provide throughout subpart N.
- Add specific prohibitions and examples in § 630.1403.
- Clarify that administrative leave can be used during a RIF notice period when appropriate.
- Clarify that resignation-withdrawal decisions are committed to agency discretion and that accepted deferred-resignation benefits can justify denial.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice ties the proposal to workforce realignment, deferred resignation programs, and other personnel-management uses.
- The notice also contains the standard compliance sections on federalism, the Regulatory Flexibility Act, the Paperwork Reduction Act, and civil justice review.

**Inference:** The proposal is best read as a codification-and-clarification package that broadens agency discretion while tightening some leave and resignation boundaries.

**Evidence Quotes**
- OPM proposes to amend its regulations to more fully address the appropriate uses of administrative leave.
- An agency may, in its sole and exclusive discretion, accept or decline an employee's request to withdraw his or her resignation.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally ordinary, but it concentrates discretion in agency decisionmakers and leaves several boundary rules under-specified.
