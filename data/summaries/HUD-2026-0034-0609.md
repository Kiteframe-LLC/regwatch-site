# Public Summary: HUD-2026-0034-0609

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** HUD proposes to remove 24 CFR 1.4(b)(2)(i), remove the ‘or effect’ language in 24 CFR 1.4(b)(3), remove 24 CFR 1.4(b)(6), remove 24 CFR 1.4(c)(2), redesignate related paragraphs, and align the employment cross-reference with Executive Order 14173. It also reopens comments on related topics from the January 2026 proposal.

**Affected Entities**
- Recipients and applicants for HUD Federal financial assistance
- Public housing agencies, State and local governments, and other HUD-funded entities
- HUD enforcement and compliance staff
- People and communities affected by decisions in HUD-assisted programs, including groups protected by Title VI

**Statutory Authority**
- Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, 42 U.S.C. 2000d–2000d-3
- HUD authority cited at 42 U.S.C. 3535(d) and 24 CFR part 1

**Observed Fact Vs Inference**
**Observed Fact:** The proposed text narrows the regulatory language to intentional-discrimination concepts and removes provisions expressly referring to effects.

**Inference:** The practical result would likely reduce the role of disparate-impact analysis in HUD’s Title VI compliance work, although the notice does not specify every enforcement consequence.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is textually coherent but leaves material evidence, distributional, and implementation questions unresolved.
