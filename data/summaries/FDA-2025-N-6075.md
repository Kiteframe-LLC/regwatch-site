# Public Summary: FDA-2025-N-6075-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** FDA proposes to amend 21 CFR part 207 to create a single-establishment registration pathway for qualifying distributed manufacturing establishments and to clarify foreign-establishment registration and drug-listing duties.

**Affected Entities**
- Drug manufacturers using hub-and-spoke distributed manufacturing
- Domestic and foreign establishments that manufacture, repack, relabel, or salvage drugs for import or commercial distribution
- FDA registration, listing, and inspection programs

**Statutory Authority:** FD&C Act sections 201, 301, 501, 502, 505, 510, 512, 701, 704, 801, and 1003; PHS Act sections 351, 361, and 368; and section 2511(b) of the PREVENT Pandemics Act.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Qualifying DMEs could register as one establishment, with additions, removals, and mobile-unit relocations handled through updates; foreign establishments would be expressly covered when their drugs are imported or offered for import, including where further foreign processing occurs.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice describes a hub-and-spoke model with one quality unit and a unified pharmaceutical quality system.
- The proposal includes definitions, initial-registration and update requirements, mobile-DMU provisions, foreign registration and listing amendments, severability, and PRA analysis.
- FDA estimates annualized net costs of approximately $0.53 million as a primary estimate at a three percent discount rate and identifies greater supply-chain visibility as an unquantified benefit.

**Inference:** The proposal is primarily an administrative modernization and supply-chain-visibility rule; the central public-interest question is whether consolidation preserves sufficiently granular, timely, and reconstructable information.

**Evidence Quotes**

- improves the Agency's visibility into the drug supply chain

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** Consolidated DME registration
  
  **Why It Matters:** Burden relief is valuable only if FDA can still identify each unit, location, product, and quality-system relationship.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Require a stable unit-level identifier and publish the minimum update fields and timing rules.
- **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** Mobile-DMU relocations
  
  **Why It Matters:** Rapid moves create a timing problem for registration accuracy and inspection targeting.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Define advance-notice exceptions, update deadlines, and when relocation is too material for an expedited update.
- **Priority:** medium
  
  **Issue:** Foreign-establishment data quality
  
  **Why It Matters:** Registration and listing expansion will help only if records are complete and reconcilable.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Explain validation, reconciliation, and follow-up for foreign establishments in multi-stage supply chains.
- **Priority:** medium
  
  **Issue:** Small-entity and pass-through costs
  
  **Why It Matters:** Aggregate net costs may conceal concentrated effects on smaller firms or domestic entities receiving foreign cost pass-through.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Publish sensitivity tables by entity size, registration volume, and update frequency.
