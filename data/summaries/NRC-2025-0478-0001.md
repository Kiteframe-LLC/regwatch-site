# Public Summary: NRC-2025-0478-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Revise 10 CFR part 51 to modernize NRC NEPA procedures.

**Affected Entities**
- license applicants
- licensees
- petitioners for rulemaking
- Tribes and state agencies with consultation interests
- the public commenting on NRC environmental review decisions

**Statutory Authority:** NEPA as amended by recent legislation, the NRC's substantive statutory authority, relevant NEPA case law, and Executive Order 14300 and related executive orders.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The rule would narrow review scope, revise definitions, update review-level procedures, add categorical exclusions, expand flexibility for environmental submissions, and remove outdated requirements in favor of guidance such as NUREG-2270.

**Observed Facts**
- The text is an NPRM titled Implementation of the National Environmental Policy Act.
- The notice says the NRC is proposing to streamline and modernize its implementation of NEPA.
- The proposal explicitly mentions new categorical exclusions and new draft guidance NUREG-2270.

**Inference**
- The practical effect is a narrower, more guidance-heavy NEPA workflow with more line-drawing done at the outset and less in the old regulatory text.

  **Why It Matters:** A categorical exclusion is only safe if the excluded actions really are low-impact across realistic cases.
