# Public Summary: CFTC-2026-0893-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Commission proposes to add CFTC-59 to 17 CFR 146.12 and exempt it from specified Privacy Act access, amendment, disclosure-accounting, relevancy, and agency-rule provisions. The selected document reopens comments after a Regulations.gov availability error.

**Affected Entities**
- Individuals whose information is maintained in CFTC-59
- Confidential sources and witnesses
- CFTC investigators, records staff, counsel, contractors, organizations, and commenters

**Statutory Authority:** The NPRM relies on the Privacy Act, especially 5 U.S.C. 552a(k)(2), and proposes corresponding amendments to 17 CFR part 146. It also discusses the preliminary inapplicability of CEA sections 15(a) and 15(b).

**Key Regulatory Changes**
- Add CFTC-59 to 17 CFR 146.12(h).
- Exempt CFTC-59 from the listed Privacy Act provisions, subject to section 552a(k)(2).
- Apply corresponding exemptions to CFTC Privacy Act regulations and reopen comments through CFTC-2026-0893-0002.

**Observed Facts**
- The NPRM identifies CFTC-59 as a new Insider Risk Program system of records.
- It gives reasons involving investigative integrity, sources, relevancy, and agency requirements.
- The reopening notice attributes the added period to a Regulations.gov availability error.

**Inference:** The proposal prioritizes investigative confidentiality over several transparency and correction mechanisms, but does not fully specify safeguards for inaccurate, stale, overbroad, or closed-case records.

**Evidence Quotes**
- exempt this system of records
- actual or potential insider risks
- keep confidential the identity of sources
- requirements and limitations set forth therein
