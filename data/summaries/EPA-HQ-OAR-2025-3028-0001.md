# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2025-3028-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes a Federal plan to implement the revised OSWI emission guidelines for existing units that lack an effective state or tribal plan by the federal-plan effective date.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of other solid waste incineration units
- States and Tribes with existing OSWI units subject to the revised emission guidelines
- State, local, Tribal, and delegated air agencies that will administer the plan
- Facilities and operators covered by the inventory and ICR supporting materials

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a proposed rule with a public comment deadline and a possible virtual public hearing.
- The proposal states that no additional control requirements or costs are expected beyond the 2025 revised OSWI EG.
- The supporting memoranda identify 60 OSWI units in 12 states and estimate that 59 units in 11 states will be covered under the federal plan.
- The supporting statement estimates annual burden and O&M costs for the information collection requirements tied to the rule.

**Inference**
- This is a plan-implementation NPRM that depends on a forecast about future state-plan approvals, not just on the text of the federal-plan mechanism itself.
- The most useful comment angles focus on the coverage assumptions, the no-additional-controls claim, and the implementation burden story.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is proposing a Federal plan to implement the revised emission guidelines for existing other solid waste incineration (OSWI) units.
- Presuming that State plans that have already been submitted to EPA will be approved, that any State indicating its intent to submit a plan will follow through with an approvable State plan, and that any negative declaration submitted by a State is accurate, we estimate that 59 OSWI units in 11 States will be covered by the Federal plan.
- The proposed Federal plan, which adopts the model rule standards and associated requirements in the revised EGs, would not result in any additional control requirements or impose any additional costs above those previously considered in the 2025 revised OSWI EG.
