# Public Summary: CFTC-2026-0893-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The proposal would add CFTC-59 to 17 CFR 146.12(h) and exempt it from specified Privacy Act provisions on disclosure accounting, access, amendment, relevancy, agency requirements, and agency rules.

**Affected Entities:** Individuals whose information is in CFTC-59, CFTC staff and contractors, the Commission, and confidential sources.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice invokes 5 U.S.C. 552a(k)(2), the Privacy Act, the APA, and Part 146 authority.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The exemption would reduce access, amendment, and related transparency for CFTC-59 records.

**Observed Facts**
- The NPRM identifies the system, the proposed statutory subsection, and the specific Privacy Act provisions at issue.
- The stated reasons are protection of investigative integrity and confidentiality of sources.
- The NPRM requests comment on the justification and scope of the exemptions.

**Inference:** The key comment question is whether the record supports the categorical scope and identifies adequate accuracy, audit, and delayed-review safeguards.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The Commission is proposing to exempt this system of records from certain provisions of the Privacy Act because the records are compiled to investigate actual or potential insider risks.
- The Commission requests comment on the justification for and scope of the proposed CFTC–59 exemptions.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Categorical scope:** Compare delayed, partial, segregated, and post-investigation review so source protection does not eliminate more individual review rights than necessary.
- **Factual basis:** Provide a provision-by-provision risk explanation and non-sensitive aggregate examples supporting the breadth of the exemption.
- **Accuracy safeguards:** Explain retention limits, correction supplements, audit logs, provenance, and review after investigative sensitivity ends.
- **Application criteria:** Define the record categories, investigative stages, and approval process for applying the exemption.
