# Public Summary: OPM_FRDOC_0001-4630

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Conform excepted-service schedules to recent Executive orders, replace legacy part 302 hiring lists and orders with a priority-centered framework, authorize specified Pathways conversions, and revise ALJ appointment and RIF rules.

**Affected Entities**
- Federal agencies and human-resources offices
- Applicants, preference eligibles, veterans, and employees with restoration or priority-consideration rights
- Pathways Participants, Schedule Policy/Career or Schedule G employees, and administrative law judges

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies on 5 U.S.C. 1302(c), 3315, 3317-3320, 3502, 3105, 7521, related statutes, Civil Service Rules, and recent Executive orders.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Key changes are the part 302 priority framework, annual rather than monthly schedule-authority publication, Schedule E, Policy/Career, and G provisions, Pathways conversions, and revised ALJ RIF placement rules.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice invites comment on priority procedures, transition, costs, and implementation.
- It provides a first-year cost estimate and preserves other statutory ALJ rights.

**Inference:** The package is interconnected, so transition rules, consistent agency documentation, and public reconstructability matter.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is broadly coherent but depends on decentralized agency implementation, reduced publication frequency, and later guidance without a fully specified common record, transition model, or monitoring design.
