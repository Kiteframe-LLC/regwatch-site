# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2024-0078-0003

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA announces an intended approach for final 2024 annual PM2.5 designations and invites comment. Area-level details are in 120-day letters, datasets, and related technical materials.

**Affected Entities**
- States and the District of Columbia
- U.S. territories
- Tribal governments, including Tribes with treatment in a similar manner as a state and certain potentially affected Tribes
- Counties, metropolitan areas, monitors, and facilities located in or contributing to areas that may be designated nonattainment
- Residents and communities affected by PM2.5 exposure and future state implementation planning

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act section 107(d)(1), with the 2024 annual PM2.5 NAAQS at 9.0 micrograms per cubic meter under 40 CFR 50.20 and design-value procedures in 40 CFR part 50, Appendix N.

**Key Regulatory Change:** EPA is moving from initial designations based generally on 2021-2023 data toward final designations using 2023-2025 data and 120-day notifications.

**Core Tradeoff:** EPA gains a current process, while commenters must reconstruct area-level evidence from dispersed materials during a short window.

**Observed Facts**
- The revised standard is 9.0 micrograms per cubic meter; initial submissions generally used 2021-2023 data.
- EPA intends to use 2023-2025 data and sent intended-modification notifications around October 2, 2026.

**Inference:** The main comment leverage point is whether the public record makes each intended designation reproducible before final action.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA intends to rely on 2023-2025 design values.
- Information is provided in 120-day letters and docket datasets.
- EPA invites public input during a 30-day comment period.
