# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2025-0124-2920

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to rescind the 2015 findings and repeal section 111 GHG standards for fossil fuel-fired EGUs, with a narrower alternative for selected requirements.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of fossil fuel-fired steam generating units, IGCC facilities, and stationary combustion turbines
- Electric utilities, power-sector investors, and air regulators
- Electricity consumers, workers, and communities near affected facilities

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act section 111(b)(1)(A), section 111(b), and section 111(d).

**Key Regulatory Change:** Remove GHG-specific section 111 controls while retaining other requirements.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a supplemental NPRM distinct from a concurrent final rule.
- The supporting RIA projects CO2 increases and reports $19 billion in present-value compliance-cost savings at 3 percent and $9.6 billion at 7 percent.

**Inference:** The broad rollback depends on both section 111 interpretation and an impact record that is not yet current.

**Evidence Quotes**
- CAA section 111 does not authorize the EPA to regulate emissions from power plants in response to global climate change concerns.
- eliminating all GHG emissions from the U.S. power sector as an overly conservative proxy
- In absence of updated baseline modeling
