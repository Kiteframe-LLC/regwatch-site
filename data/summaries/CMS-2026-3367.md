# Public Summary: CMS-2026-3367-0002

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts:** The NPRM would require earlier and more frequent Exchange verification, pause APTC and CSR payment for specified unresolved DMIs, require annual confirmation for subsequent-year assistance, and create pended-plan and first-of-month effective-date rules. It cites ACA sections 1311, 1401, 1402, and 1411, WFTC section 71303, and the existing part 155 authority citation.

**Regulatory Action:** The affected entities are Marketplace applicants and enrollees, Exchanges, QHP issuers, agents, brokers, assisters, State Medicaid and CHIP agencies, and tax filers.

**Inference:** The proposal shifts eligibility administration toward verification as a gateway to affordability and coverage, while using pended selection and exceptions as mitigations.

**Core Tradeoff:** Stronger program integrity may reduce improper subsidy payment, but delayed or erroneous verification can create affordability, continuity, administrative, and federalism costs.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is coherent in purpose and legal structure, but it makes verification timing a consequential gateway to affordability and coverage before fully documenting error correction, subgroup effects, and implementation readiness.

## Who Is Affected

- Marketplace applicants and current enrollees seeking APTC or CSRs
- consumers with citizenship, lawful-presence, residency, income, family-size, or other coverage DMIs
- State Exchanges and Exchanges on the federal platform
- qualified health plan issuers
- agents, brokers, web-brokers, and enrollment assisters
- State Medicaid and CHIP agencies
- tax filers and households affected by APTC reconciliation

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **DMI subsidy gate:** Withholding APTC and CSR can make coverage unaffordable before correction. Ask for DMI accuracy and resolution evidence and a protected-payment or staged-review option.
- **Annual confirmation:** A missed August-December contact window can terminate assistance for an otherwise eligible enrollee. Ask for a grace period, targeted confirmation, and assisted/offline options.
- **Readiness:** Incomplete systems or notices could create inconsistent enrollment and effective dates. Ask for readiness milestones and a phased or contingency path for Exchanges and issuers.
- **Effective dates:** A one-day difference around the 15th can change the coverage month and binder amount. Ask for examples, sensitivity analysis, and a narrow grace or appeal rule for processing delays.
- **Monitoring:** The control architecture's error and coverage effects are uncertain. Ask for public metrics, periodic review, escalation, and corrective action for DMI reversals, subsidy interruptions, and subgroup disparities.
