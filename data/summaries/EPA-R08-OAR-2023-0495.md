# Public Summary: EPA-R08-OAR-2023-0495-0065

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to repeal the 2024 partial approval/partial disapproval of North Dakota's second-implementation-period regional-haze SIP and approve the previously disapproved long-term-strategy, RPG, and FLM-consultation elements.

**Affected Entities**
- North Dakota Department of Environmental Quality
- facilities and owners evaluated in the State SIP four-factor analysis
- Federal Land Managers and Class I area managers
- EPA Region 8
- communities and visitors affected by visibility conditions

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 169A and 169B and 40 CFR 51.308; the proposal also discusses EPA authority to reconsider the 2024 action.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would remove the practical consequences of the 2024 disapproval and approve the State plan as submitted.

**Observed Facts**
- The 2024 final rule disapproved 40 CFR 51.308(f)(2), (f)(3), and (i).
- Petitions for reconsideration were received in January and February 2025; EPA granted reconsideration April 30, 2025.
- The Eighth Circuit petitions for review were held in abeyance pending reconsideration.

**Inference:** The most consequential issue is not a new emissions limit but whether EPA's change in position is transparent, technically reproducible, and consistent with the statutory regional-haze framework.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is proposing full approval of North Dakota's 2022 SIP submission.
- In the 2024 final action, the EPA disapproved 40 CFR 51.308(f)(2) ... (f)(3) ... and 40 CFR 51.308(i).
