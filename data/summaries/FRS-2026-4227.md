# Public Summary: FRS-2026-4227-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The proposal would add a letter-based Board approval process for an insured State member bank's payment stablecoin subsidiary.

**Affected Entities**
- Insured State member banks that seek approval for a payment stablecoin subsidiary
- Proposed Board-supervised permitted payment stablecoin issuers and their officers, directors, and principal shareholders
- Consortium and multi-bank structures that may seek coordinated approval
- Customers and market participants affected by the safety, redemption, reserve, and compliance controls of approved issuers

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies on section 5 of the GENIUS Act, 12 U.S.C. 5904, and related Federal Reserve authority.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The specialized track would include completeness notice, a 120-day decision period, deemed approval, denial reasons, and a hearing path.

**Core Tradeoff:** Predictable procedure versus enough discretion to evaluate safety, reserves, redemption, management, technology, and compliance.

**Observed Facts**
- The application must include business, financial, governance, compliance, and background information.
- The Board may request more information and impose approval conditions.
- The Board estimates 405 annual burden hours and $30,112 total cost.

**Inference:** The procedural gateway can determine whether an issuer reaches substantive review and begins operations.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent, but the discretionary completeness and condition-setting architecture can weaken the predictability and accountability of the statutory review clock.
