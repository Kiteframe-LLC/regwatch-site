# Public Summary: IRS-2026-1223-0001

## What This Document Does

**What Changes:** The proposal adds annual QOF information-return duties, QOZB-to-QOF statements, investor-disposition statements, related broker reporting, penalty rules, and procedures for inadvertent certification revocation and voluntary QOF decertification.

**Affected Entities:** QOFs, applicable QOZBs, QOF investors disposing of interests, brokers handling covered dispositions, and tax professionals or service providers that support those entities.

**Why It Matters:** The proposal translates new statutory reporting requirements into recurring operational duties and can affect access to opportunity-zone tax benefits, compliance costs, and the information available to the IRS and investors.

**Observed Fact:** The notice estimates annual paperwork burdens for both QOFs and QOZBs and proposes a $500-per-day penalty for specified QOF reporting failures, subject to stated caps and reasonable-cause relief.

**Inference:** The most useful comments will improve reporting design and implementation evidence without disputing statutory reporting obligations.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal has a coherent statutory reporting structure; its main weakness is uncertainty about implementation burden and the treatment of correctable electronic errors.
