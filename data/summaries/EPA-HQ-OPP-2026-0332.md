# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2026-0332-0006

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- EPA announces four petition filings for new or modified pesticide residue tolerances or an exemption.
- The notice lists petition dockets, requested actions, methods, contacts, and a comment opportunity.
- It makes no final tolerance decision and says sufficiency review is incomplete.

**Inference:** Meaningful comments depend on access to the petition-specific summaries and supporting data.

**Regulatory Action:** Filing notice and request for comment under FFDCA section 408(d)(3), before substantive evaluation.

**Affected Entities:** Petitioners, registrants, growers, processors, importers, retailers, consumers, laboratories, and other parties affected by residue limits.

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(2)-(4), 21 U.S.C. 346a, and 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No final tolerance changes are made; EPA may later vary a commodity name or level.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time or whether the data supports granting the pesticide petitions.
- Additional data may be needed before EPA can make a final determination on these pesticide petitions.
