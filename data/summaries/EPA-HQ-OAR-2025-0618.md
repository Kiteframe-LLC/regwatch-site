# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2025-0618-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to revise NSR definitions so certain non-emitting construction activities can occur before NSR permit issuance, while keeping permit and emissions-control requirements in place for actual source construction.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators planning new or modified major stationary sources
- State, local, Tribal, and delegated air permitting authorities
- Projects in PSD, NNSR, SIP, TIP, FIP, and Tribal NSR programs

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a proposed rule with a live public-comment deadline and a request for public hearing.
- The proposal would revise begin actual construction, add pollutant-emitting activities, and adjust Tribal NSR terminology.
- The notice explicitly asks whether the carveout list should be broadened, narrowed, or moved into source definitions.
- The notice also asks whether approved plans must be revised if the proposal is finalized.

**Inference:** This is a program-wide NSR definitional rewrite, not a narrow housekeeping change, because it affects when work may start across several permit programs.

**Evidence Quotes**

- pollutant-emitting activities
- all aspects of this proposed action
- existing approved plans
