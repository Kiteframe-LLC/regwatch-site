# Public Summary: FTC-2026-1024-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FTC is soliciting comments on a petition asking for an interpretive rule about consumer freedom of choice and the alleged market-distorting effects of deceptive information. The notice does not propose rule text.

**Affected Entities**
- consumers who rely on truthful information when making market choices
- firms whose advertising, disclosures, or interface design could be implicated
- consumer advocates and public-interest researchers
- FTC staff and Commissioners evaluating whether to open a broader inquiry
- market participants that may be affected if the petition leads to later guidance or enforcement

**Statutory Authority:** 15 U.S.C. 46; 15 U.S.C. 57a; 5 U.S.C. 601 note; FTC Rule 1.31(f).

**Key Regulatory Change:** No regulatory change yet; the notice opens a comment window on whether the Commission should take up the petition.

**Observed Facts**

- The notice says the petition is online and open for public comment.
- The notice says the Commission will not consider the petition's merits until the comment period closes.

**Inference:** The notice is best read as petition intake with a sparse factual record, so a useful comment should supply concrete examples or ask the FTC to narrow the issue.
