# Public Summary: FDIC-2026-1191-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FDIC proposes under sections 7 and 9 of the FDI Act to amend 12 CFR part 327 by raising the small/large institution threshold to $30 billion, indexing it every four years to CPI-W, lowering assessment rate schedules, adding a resolution readiness adjustment, and removing obsolete provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- insured depository institutions near the small/large threshold
- large and highly complex institutions subject to the scorecard methodology
- small institutions and insured branches of foreign banks
- institutions considering VDR testing or prescribed data access for the RRA
- commenters and service providers involved in resolution-readiness compliance

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the current threshold has been in place since 2006 and would be adjusted every four years for inflation.
- The notice estimates that about 76 institutions would move from the large-institution method to the small-institution method.
- The notice also says the proposal would lower initial base assessment rate schedules and create an RRA credit tied to testing and data access.

**Inference:** The rule is a recoding of assessment classes and incentives rather than a wholly new assessment system; the strongest comments will focus on where the threshold is drawn, how often it is updated, and how the RRA works in practice.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The FDIC, under its general rulemaking authority in section 9 of the FDI Act, and its specific authority under section 7 of the FDI Act...
- The FDIC is proposing to update the asset-based threshold ... from $10 billion ... to $30 billion.
- The proposed rule would amend the risk-based assessment system to include a downward adjustment, the RRA.
