# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-0110-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA is seeking comment on a group of pesticide petitions that would establish, modify, or exempt tolerance requirements for residues on specific commodities.

**Affected Entities**
- Pesticide petitioners and registrants
- Growers and commodity groups for the listed crops
- Food processors and handlers that rely on tolerance decisions
- State, Tribal, and Federal reviewers who will later enforce the tolerance framework
- Public commenters concerned with residue, exposure, and enforcement consequences

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with related references to 40 CFR 180.7.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is styled as a notice of filing petitions and request for comment.
- EPA says it has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data yet.
- The notice discusses both tolerance changes and tolerance exemptions across multiple petitions.
- The notice flags commodity vocabulary, harmonization, and related-commodity issues as matters commenters should consider.

**Inference:** The record is about record-building for multiple petition outcomes, not about finalizing a single uniform regulatory change.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Id:** angle_sufficiency_matrix
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Posture:** constructive
  
  **Issue:** EPA should publish a petition-by-petition sufficiency matrix.
  
  **Why It Matters:** That would let the public respond to the real evidence gaps instead of guessing at EPA's threshold.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Identify which data types are still needed for Ascr#18, melatonin, and sulfoxaflor.
  
  **Supporting Evidence**
  - Additional data may be needed before EPA can make a final determination on these pesticide petitions.
- **Id:** angle_related_commodities
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Posture:** constructive
  
  **Issue:** EPA should explain how it will screen related commodities and downstream residues.
  
  **Why It Matters:** A tolerance or exemption can be incomplete if connected commodities are not reviewed.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** List the related commodities reviewed for each petition and the reason for any exclusions.
  
  **Supporting Evidence**
  - Commenters should consider whether use of the pesticide on a crop for which a tolerance is requested may also require a tolerance on related commodities.
- **Id:** angle_name_crosswalk
  
  **Priority:** moderate
  
  **Posture:** constructive
  
  **Issue:** EPA should show how any commodity-name or tolerance-level edits will change the final scope.
  
  **Why It Matters:** Small wording changes can have real enforcement consequences.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Provide a before-and-after crosswalk for any final terminology changes.
  
  **Supporting Evidence**
  - EPA may determine that it is appropriate to vary the commodity name for consistency with EPA's Food and Feed Commodity Vocabulary.
- **Id:** angle_petition_specificity
  
  **Priority:** moderate
  
  **Posture:** constructive
  
  **Issue:** EPA should keep the rationale separate for each petition track.
  
  **Why It Matters:** The residue, method, and exposure stories are not the same for Ascr#18, melatonin, and sulfoxaflor.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Issue petition-specific responses or a clear comparison table before final action.
  
  **Supporting Evidence**
  - PP 3F9099
  - PP 4F9133
  - PP 6F9228
