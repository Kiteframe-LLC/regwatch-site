# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3138-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal revises the Table of TV Allotments for St. George, Utah.
- It replaces UHF channel 21 with VHF channel 9 in the Table while retaining channel 18.
- The notice says KMYU(TV) would use its current licensed channel-9 parameters.

**Inference**
- The practical effect is to preserve the station's existing operating configuration rather than require the contemplated move to channel 21.

**Regulatory Action:** Revise 47 CFR 73.622(j) so the St. George entry reads channel 9,* and 18.

**Affected Entities**

- nearby television broadcasters and potential channel-allotment users
- FCC Media Bureau and technical staff

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites 47 U.S.C. §§ 154, 155, 301, 303, 307, 309, 310, 334, 336, and 339.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The Table would list VHF channel 9 instead of UHF channel 21 for St. George, while retaining channel 18.
