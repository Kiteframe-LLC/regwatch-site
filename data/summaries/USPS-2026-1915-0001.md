# Public Summary: USPS-2026-1915-0001

## What This Document Does

**Rule Summary:** The Postal Service proposes to revise Publication 52 standards for mailing live animals. It would bar reuse of fiberboard containers, set minimum container construction, require every container in a strapped bundle to carry its own address label and sequential number, and require direct recipient addressing on adult-bird packaging.

**Policy Problem:** USPS says the changes are intended to improve package integrity and identification during processing and handling, particularly when containers degrade or become separated.

**Affected Entities**
- Mailers and breeders shipping live animals
- Recipients of live-animal shipments
- Postal employees handling perishable and live-animal mail
- Manufacturers and sellers of shipping containers

**Key Mechanisms**
- No reuse of fiberboard containers for live animals
- Minimum 275-pound test double-wall weather-resistant fiberboard or equivalent
- Individual addressing and sequential numbering for bundled containers
- Direct recipient name and address on adult-bird packaging
