# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-0126-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA is announcing multiple pesticide petition requests for new tolerances, tolerance increases, tolerance removals, and tolerance exemptions for residues on food and feed commodities.

**Affected Entities**
- Petitioners seeking new or revised tolerances
- Growers, importers, and handlers that rely on the requested tolerances
- Laboratories and enforcement staff that would need to measure the relevant residues
- Consumers exposed to residues in the food supply

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with references to 40 CFR 180.7 and 40 CFR part 180.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The notice opens comment on a set of commodity-specific requests rather than finalizing a single tolerance decision.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice lists multiple petitioners, multiple commodities, and multiple analytical methods.
- EPA says it has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data.
- EPA says a final tolerance or exemption may vary from the petitioner’s request.

**Inferences**
- Comment leverage is strongest where the notice gives only a petition-level summary and not the underlying residue package.
- The public record is broad enough to support comment drafting, but not deep enough to reproduce EPA’s final tolerance analysis.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time or whether the data supports granting the pesticide petitions.
- Additional data may be needed before EPA can make a final determination on these pesticide petitions.
- EPA may establish a final tolerance or tolerance exemption that 'may vary from that sought by the petitioner.'

- **Id:** angle_methods
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** Ask whether the cited analytical methods are validated for each commodity and residue definition.
  
  **Why It Matters:** A tolerance is only enforceable if the method works in the relevant matrix and actually measures the target residue.

  **Supporting Evidence**
