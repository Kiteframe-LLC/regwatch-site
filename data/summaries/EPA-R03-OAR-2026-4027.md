# Public Summary: EPA-R03-OAR-2026-4027-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve ACHD's negative declaration and amend 40 CFR part 62 to record that Allegheny County has no existing LMWC subject to the section 111(d)/129 guidelines. The declaration would serve in lieu of a state plan.

**Observed Facts:** ACHD's March 25, 2026 letter states that it found no covered LMWC; the notice provides the CAA/regulatory framework and says the action imposes no additional requirements.

**Inference:** This is a routine no-source action whose central comment question is whether the absence finding is documented, correctly matched to federal criteria, and paired with a correction path if conditions change.

**Evidence Quotes:** “there are no existing large municipal waste combustors”; “may submit a negative declaration in lieu of a State plan.”
