# Public Summary: FCC-2026-1750-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Exclude Covered List entities from automatic domestic interstate telecommunications authority under blanket section 214 authority, while considering revocation, interconnection limits, and other adjacent national-security measures.

**Affected Entities**
- Entities on the Covered List that may seek domestic interstate telecommunications authority
- Current blanket authority holders that could face revocation proceedings
- Corporate affiliates, subsidiaries, assignees, and transferees connected to Covered List entities
- Other telecommunications carriers that interconnect with or rely on affected entities
- Consumers and small entities that could face switching or compliance costs

**Statutory Authority:** Sections 1-4, 201, 214, 251, and 301-303 of the Communications Act.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The FCC would replace default blanket authorization with exclusion-based review for Covered List entities, and it asks whether to expand the reach of that exclusion or add downstream restrictions.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice proposes to amend 47 CFR 63.01(a) so Covered List entities are no longer automatically authorized under blanket section 214 authority.
- The proposal is prospective, but the FCC also asks whether to revoke existing authority and whether to apply the exclusion to current and future affiliates and subsidiaries.
- The FCC separately asks about interconnection restrictions, foreign-adversary expansions, and possible unlicensed-wireless consequences.

**Inference**
- This is a national-security-focused effort to move domestic telecom entry away from generalized blanket permission and toward affirmative review for higher-risk entities.
- The main comment leverage is not whether national security matters, but whether the FCC has shown the fit between existing Covered List determinations and each downstream consequence it wants to impose.

**Evidence Quotes**
- We propose to amend § 63.01 ... to exclude, on a prospective basis, any entities identified on the Covered List.
- We further propose that any entity so excluded should be able to submit an individual application seeking affirmative approval.
- We tentatively conclude that section 201(b) provides direct authority for the Commission to prohibit telecommunications carriers from interconnecting with entities on the Covered List.
