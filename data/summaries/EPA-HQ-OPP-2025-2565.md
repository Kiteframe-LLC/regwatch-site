# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-2565-0003

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to increase the afidopyropen tolerance for orange, subgroup 10-10A from 0.15 ppm to 0.30 ppm.

**Affected Entities**
- BASF Corporation as petitioner and likely beneficiary of the use expansion
- Growers and handlers using afidopyropen on citrus
- Consumers exposed through food residues

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with implementing references to 40 CFR 180.7 and 40 CFR part 180.700.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would raise an existing citrus tolerance and tie it to the petition record summarized in the docket.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says EPA is taking public comments before responding to the petitioner.

**Inferences**
- The practical leverage for commenters is in the supporting petition record rather than the bare notice language.

**Evidence Quotes**
- requests to amend (increase) the existing afidopyropen tolerance established in 40 CFR part 180.700 in or on Orange, subgroup 10-10A from 0.15 ppm to 0.3 ppm
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data
