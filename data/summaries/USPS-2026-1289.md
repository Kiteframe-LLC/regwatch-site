# Public Summary: USPS-2026-1289-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Postal Service proposes a new DMM section governing ballot mail for federal elections, including envelope standards, optional pre-mailing notice, participation lists, and verification before acceptance.

**Affected Entities**
- State chief election officials and local election offices
- Authorized ballot mailers and mail service providers
- Voters receiving or returning federal election ballots by mail
- Postal Service employees and acceptance facilities

**Statutory Authority:** 39 U.S.C. 401 and 404, along with the Postal Service’s referenced rulemaking authority and a presidential executive order.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal adds a ballot-mail portal, a state-specific participation list, barcode-linked enrollment, and pre-acceptance verification of outbound ballot mail.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is labeled Proposed Rule and says USPS is amending DMM 705.
- The text says states may submit enrollment information for a Mail-In and Absentee Participation List.
- The text says outbound ballot mail will be reviewed before acceptance.

**Inference:** This is not just a mailing-standards cleanup; it creates a data-driven workflow that reaches into election-mail logistics and acceptance timing.

**Evidence Quotes**
- USPS says it is proposing standards for ballot mail.
- The notice says USPS will review outbound ballot mail before acceptance.
