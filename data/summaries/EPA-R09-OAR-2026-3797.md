# Public Summary: EPA-R09-OAR-2026-3797-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to partially approve Arizona's 2012 PM2.5 infrastructure SIP submission for PSD-related Clean Air Act sections 110(a)(2)(C), 110(a)(2)(D)(i)(II), 110(a)(2)(D)(ii), and 110(a)(2)(J) in ADEQ, MCAQD, and PCAQCD jurisdictions, and partially disapprove it for PDEQ, while withdrawing the corresponding portion of the 2024 proposal.

**Affected Entities**
- Arizona and its air permitting authorities
- ADEQ, MCAQD, PCAQCD, and PDEQ permitting jurisdictions
- Major stationary sources subject to PSD permitting
- EPA Region IX and members of the public relying on SIP and permitting oversight

**Observed Facts**
- The proposed approval is tied to SIP-approved provisions regulating PM2.5 and precursors and PM2.5 PSD increments.
- PDEQ is described as using a comprehensive federal PSD implementation plan for PM2.5 and precursors.
- EPA says the action imposes no requirements beyond state law and is not a significant regulatory action.

**Inference:** The practical stakes are concentrated in the legal characterization of existing permitting authority and the transparency of EPA's changed interpretation, rather than in a new emission limit imposed directly on sources.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is now proposing partial approval and partial disapproval ... based on a change in policy.
- If finalized, this disapproval ... would not create any new consequences ... as the county agency already implements the EPA's Federal PSD program.
