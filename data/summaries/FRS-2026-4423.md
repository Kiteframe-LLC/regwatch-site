# Public Summary: FRS-2026-3334-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Board proposes to restate and revise Regulation O so that insider-lending limits, threshold amounts, control presumptions, valuation methods, and related disclosure and recordkeeping rules are modernized and aligned with newer statutory and regulatory concepts.

**Affected Entities**
- Member banks and other insured depository institutions subject to Regulation O
- Executive officers, directors, principal shareholders, and related interests
- Portfolio companies and fund complexes affected by the passive-control proposal
- Banks that make correspondent loans, hold insider debt securities, or enter derivative and securities-financing transactions with insiders
- Community banks and other smaller institutions that may feel the threshold changes differently from larger firms

**Statutory Authority:** Sections 22(g) and 22(h) of the Federal Reserve Act, 12 U.S.C. 375a and 375b, the Dodd-Frank Act amendments to insider-credit definitions, and the Board’s supervisory authority over Regulation O.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would raise several dollar thresholds, index them every five years using nominal GDP, create a passive-fund-complex carveout from the control presumption, add valuation rules for derivative and securities-financing exposures, codify several longstanding interpretations, and reorganize the rule for readability.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is labeled a notice of proposed rulemaking and expressly invites comment on all aspects of the proposal.
- The text says the Board has reviewed Regulation O and is restating it in full rather than amending scattered sections piecemeal.
- The notice includes sections on economic analysis, Regulatory Flexibility Act treatment, paperwork reduction, and plain-language revisions.

**Inference:** This is a broad modernization NPRM rather than a narrow technical correction, because several separate substantive interpretations are being codified at the same time.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The Board is inviting public comment on proposed amendments to Regulation O.
- In view of the extensive proposed changes and reorganization, the Board is restating Regulation O in its entirety as amended.
