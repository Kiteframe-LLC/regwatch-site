# Public Summary: FDA-2026-N-2886-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** FDA proposes to revise terminology in 21 CFR Parts 10, 56, 106, 201, 251, 310, 312, 314, 329, 600, 803, 862, 866, 870, 882, and 1114 by removing the word gender, replacing it with sex, or deleting the reference, with related editorial changes.

**Affected Entities**
- FDA program offices and reviewers
- Drug, biologic, device, and tobacco-product sponsors and manufacturers
- Institutional review boards, investigators, and clinical researchers
- Entities responsible for adverse-event, clinical-validation, and product-reporting systems
- Patients, study participants, and members of populations represented in regulated data

**Observed Facts**
- The notice lists 26 CFR provisions for revision.
- The stated authority includes the FD&C Act, PHS Act, Administrative Procedure Act, and other listed statutes.
- The notice characterizes the changes as editorial, predicts no measurable industry change, and estimates zero quantified costs, savings, transfers, and public-health benefit loss.
- The rule text changes examples and data descriptors in clinical and product-reporting provisions, including references to age, sex, race, or ethnicity.

**Inference**
- The proposal is primarily a terminology and regulatory-text alignment rule, but its provisions touch data collection and reporting contexts where terminology can affect meaning.
- The central comment leverage is not whether editorial correction is permissible in the abstract, but whether FDA has demonstrated semantic continuity and workable implementation for every affected provision.

**Evidence Quotes**
- remove the term gender wherever it appears
- does not impact industry practices
- We do not expect any loss of public health benefits as a result of this rule.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal has a clear terminology objective, but it relies on a broad editorial characterization across diverse data and reporting programs without a visible semantic-continuity or implementation showing.
