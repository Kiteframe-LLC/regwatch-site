# Public Summary: NHTSA-2026-1519-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NHTSA proposes targeted FMVSS No. 108 changes for integral-beam testing, vertical headlamp placement, ADS vehicle controls, telltales, and optional cyan ADS marker lamps.

**Affected Entities:** Vehicle and lighting manufacturers, ADS developers and operators, State authorities, and road users.

**Statutory Authority:** 49 U.S.C. 322, 30111, 30115, 30117, and 30166; delegation at 49 CFR 1.95.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal relaxes selected design and manual-interface requirements while retaining required signaling means and setting optional marker-lamp performance limits.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is an NPRM concerning FMVSS No. 108.
- The notice has not quantified potential costs, benefits, or affected vehicles.

**Inference:** The proposal is a modernization measure, but its new visual signal depends on future adoption and road-user interpretation.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The proposed modifications would ... allow for equipping vehicles with automated driving system (ADS) marker lamps and standardizing their performance.
- The proposed ADS marker lamp criteria include ... activation, visibility, color, and photometric intensity.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Issue (high; evidence request):** Comparative safety evidence. **Why it matters:** The proposal removes requirements while relying on safety neutrality. **Question:** Can NHTSA docket tests covering voltage, beam orientation, glare, loading, and edge cases?
- **Issue (high; alternatives push):** A phased or bounded alternative. **Why it matters:** The market is still developing. **Question:** How would bounded testing, phased lamps, and diagnostics compare with the proposal?
- **Issue (high; evidence request):** Human-factors basis for cyan lamps. **Why it matters:** The lamp's purpose is road-user communication. **Question:** What recognition, confusion, ambient-light, and color-perception evidence supports the proposal?
- **Issue (moderate; collaborative clarification):** Operating-state verification. **Why it matters:** Lamp meaning depends on the monitoring state. **Question:** What tests cover transitions, failures, maintenance, fallback, and mixed-control vehicles?
- **Issue (moderate; procedural concern):** Preemption and small-entity analysis. **Why it matters:** The market picture is unquantified. **Question:** Which laws are affected, and what fleet, supplier, operator, and small-entity assumptions support the final analysis?
