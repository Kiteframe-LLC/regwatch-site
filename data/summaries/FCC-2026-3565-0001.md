# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3565-0001

## What This Document Does

The FCC proposes to turn the Robocall Mitigation Database into a more detailed and consequential accountability system. Providers would submit expanded identity, ownership, service, mitigation, and third-party information; keep filings current; undergo screening; and face expedited removal, re-entry restrictions, and traffic-acceptance consequences. The tradeoff is stronger traceability and deterrence versus privacy, due-process, implementation, and false-positive risks.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal coherently targets RMD integrity, but it may convert a data system into a high-impact gate without equally detailed accuracy and review safeguards.

## Who Is Affected

- domestic and foreign voice service providers
- wireline, wireless, VoIP resellers, MVNOs, gateway and intermediate providers
- cloud communications, call-center, platform, and dialing businesses
- third-party call analytics, STIR/SHAKEN, KYC/KYUP, and filing vendors
- downstream carriers and providers that must accept or block traffic
- end users, public safety answering points, and communities affected by illegal or mistakenly blocked calls
- small businesses and individual principals whose information may be collected or disclosed
