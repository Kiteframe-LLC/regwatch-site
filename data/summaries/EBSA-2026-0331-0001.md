# Public Summary: EBSA-2026-0331-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Create an optional additional safe harbor allowing group health plans to use notice-and-access and electronic delivery for recurring ERISA disclosures.

**Affected Entities**
- Group health plan administrators
- Participants and beneficiaries
- Third-party administrators and disclosure vendors
- Older, rural, low-income, and digitally limited participants

**Observed Facts**
- This proposed rule sets forth a new, additional safe harbor ... to furnish documents and information ... using electronic media.
- Individuals who prefer to receive these disclosures on paper will be able to request paper copies and to opt out of electronic delivery entirely.
- The notice proposes amendments to 29 CFR parts 2520 and 2560.

**Inference:** The rule shifts the default delivery channel for qualifying plans while preserving formal paper and opt-out pathways; practical access will depend on those pathways working reliably.

**Evidence Quotes**
- This proposed rule sets forth a new, additional safe harbor ... to furnish documents and information ... using electronic media.
- Individuals who prefer to receive these disclosures on paper will be able to request paper copies and to opt out of electronic delivery entirely.
- The Department estimates that the proposed rule would result in cost savings of approximately $402 million per year.
