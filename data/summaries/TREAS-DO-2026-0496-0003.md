# Public Summary: TREAS-DO-2026-0496-0003

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Treasury proposes 12 CFR part 1523 to implement GENIUS Act section 3 by defining issuance and related terms, limiting who may issue or make payment stablecoins available in the United States, addressing foreign issuers and extraterritorial conduct, and establishing exemptions, safe harbors, and interpretive examples.

**Affected Entities**
- Payment stablecoin issuers
- Foreign payment stablecoin issuers
- Digital asset service providers, exchanges, custodians, and intermediaries
- U.S. consumers and businesses using payment stablecoins
- State and foreign regulators

**Main Requirements**
- Only permitted issuers or qualifying registered foreign issuers may issue in the United States.
- Service providers must restrict offers, sales, and availability of noncompliant stablecoins, including foreign-issuer products.
- Participants must apply definitions, location rules, lawful-order conditions, exemptions, and safe harbors in proposed part 1523.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal has a coherent statutory-to-regulatory structure, with material uncertainty concentrated in enforcement boundaries, foreign-issuer assurance, and implementation feasibility.
