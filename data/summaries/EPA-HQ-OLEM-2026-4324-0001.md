# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OLEM-2026-4324-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Approve Wisconsin's partial permit program for CCR landfills, allowing approved state requirements to operate in lieu of corresponding federal CCR requirements while unapproved federal provisions remain effective.

**Why It Matters:** Program approval changes the primary regulatory pathway for CCR landfills in Wisconsin, making the clarity and protectiveness of the state-federal crosswalk consequential for groundwater, surface water, closure, and public participation.

**Affected Entities**
- Wisconsin CCR landfill owners and operators
- Wisconsin DNR and EPA Region 5
- Communities, Tribes, and water users near CCR facilities
- Public commenters evaluating program-level protectiveness

**Observed Facts**
- Wisconsin submitted the partial-program application on February 24, 2026.
- EPA proposes approval only for the requested landfill portion of the state program.
- EPA describes continued federal inspection and enforcement authority.

**Inference:** The highest-value comments test whether the record makes the scope, equivalence findings, implementation safeguards, and public-facing accountability reproducible.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA is proposing to approve the Wisconsin Coal Combustion Residuals partial permit program.
- only the State requirements that have been approved by EPA operate in lieu of the Federal requirements.
