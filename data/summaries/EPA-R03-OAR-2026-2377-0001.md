# Public Summary: EPA-R03-OAR-2026-2377-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve Philadelphia AMS negative declarations stating that no existing CISWI, LMWC, SMWC, or OSWI units subject to the identified Clean Air Act requirements are located within Philadelphia, and to amend 40 CFR part 62 to reflect that approval.

**Affected Entities**
- Philadelphia Air Management Services
- potential owners or operators of solid-waste-incinerator units in Philadelphia
- EPA Region III and state/local air-program administrators
- members of the public who may need to identify or challenge the scope of the negative declarations

**Statutory Authority**
- Clean Air Act section 111(d)
- Clean Air Act section 129 as described in the proposed rule
- 40 CFR 60.23(b), 60.23a(b), and 62.06

**Key Regulatory Changes**
- EPA would amend 40 CFR part 62 to record approval of the Philadelphia negative declarations.
- The approved declarations would serve in lieu of a state plan for the identified existing source categories.
- The proposal states that it imposes no additional requirements on regulated entities.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice identifies the four categories as CISWI, LMWC, SMWC, and OSWI.
- The supporting AMS letter lists several subparts and also includes unrelated oil-and-natural-gas requirements described as previously approved.
- The notice says comments are due September 28, 2026, and accepts comments through Regulations.gov or the listed EPA email address.

**Inference:** The proposal is narrow and administratively routine in form, but the reliability of the approval depends on the traceability of AMS's negative finding and a clear reconciliation of the source-category references.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is a narrow negative-declaration approval, but the available support does not fully reconcile the source-category and authority descriptions used in the declaration with the categories analyzed in the notice.
