# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-0094-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA is announcing three pesticide petition requests and opening comment on whether to grant an Ascr#18 exemption, a melatonin exemption, and a sulfoxaflor tolerance request.

**Affected Entities**
- petitioners seeking tolerance exemptions or tolerances
- growers, importers, and processors that rely on the requested residue treatment
- laboratories and enforcement staff that would need to measure or verify residues
- food-chain actors and consumers exposed to residues in treated commodities
- public commenters who can still influence the final tolerance or exemption scope

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with references to 40 CFR 180.7 and 40 CFR part 180.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The notice opens comment on petitioned tolerances and exemptions; EPA has not yet finalized any residue limits.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data.
- The melatonin petition seeks an exemption for all raw agricultural commodities without a numerical limit.
- The sulfoxaflor petition seeks a commodity-specific tolerance in pistachio at 0.5 ppm.

**Inferences**
- Comment leverage is strongest where the notice gives only a petition-level summary and not the underlying residue package.
- The public record is broad enough to support comment drafting, but not deep enough to reproduce EPA's final tolerance analysis.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time or whether the data supports granting the pesticide petitions.
- EPA may establish a final tolerance or tolerance exemption that may vary from that sought by the petitioner.
- no analytical method for measuring residues of melatonin in crops for enforcement purposes are required
- The Offline Solid-Phase Extraction and Liquid Chromatography with Tandem Mass Spectrometry Detection method is used to measure and evaluate the chemical sulfoxaflor.
