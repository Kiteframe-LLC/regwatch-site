# Public Summary: ED_FRDOC_0001-1152

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NPRM revising 34 CFR parts 600, 602, and 668 governing institutional eligibility, accreditor recognition, and student disclosures.

**Affected Entities**
- students
- institutions of higher education
- accrediting agencies
- States and public institutions
- taxpayers and Federal student-aid administrators

**Statutory Authority:** Higher Education Act provisions implemented through 34 CFR parts 600, 602, and 668; the notice also cites Executive Order 14279 as a directive.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Broad changes to accreditor entry, scope, standards, independence, outcomes, transfer credit, teach-out, transparency, and Department review.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice lists amendments across three regulatory parts.
- The notice states a September 21, 2026 comment deadline.
- The notice references a Regulatory Impact Analysis and no significant net budget impact.

**Inference:** The proposal would materially change the institutional-accreditor relationship and the Department's oversight architecture.

**Evidence Quotes**
