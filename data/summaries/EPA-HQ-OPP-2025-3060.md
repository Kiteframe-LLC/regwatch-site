# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-3060-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Notice of receipt and request for comment on pesticide petitions, including Albaugh's PP 5F9167 request to establish tolerances for glufosinate-P-ammonium and specified metabolites or degradates in numerous food, feed, and livestock commodities. The notice does not itself establish a tolerance.

**Affected Entities**
- pesticide registrants and petitioners
- farmers and growers using affected commodities
- food and feed processors and handlers
- livestock producers and consumers
- farm workers and communities potentially exposed to pesticide residues
- environmental and public-health commenters

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with the petition-data requirements described in section 408(d)(2) and 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No immediate regulatory change. A later EPA action could establish or vary tolerances for L-glufosinate-P-ammonium and related residues, including indirect or inadvertent residues.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says EPA has not fully evaluated data sufficiency or whether the data support granting the petitions.
- The supplied petition summary reports acute and chronic dietary estimates below the aPAD and cPAD, occupational PPE assumptions, no residential uses, and available enforcement methods.

**Inference:** The comment period is the appropriate point to test the scientific bridge, exposure assumptions, commodity coverage, and public availability of supporting materials before EPA makes a final tolerance decision.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time.
- Additional data may be needed before EPA can make a final determination on these pesticide petitions.
- The default assumption of 100% crop treated was applied by EPA for all crops.
