# Public Summary: CFTC-2026-1355-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Joint RFI asking whether the SEC and CFTC should clarify the lines between swaps, security-based swaps, mixed swaps, and excluded instruments, and whether alternative compliance should be possible where requirements are substantially similar.

**Affected Entities**
- swap dealers and major swap participants
- security-based swap dealers and major security-based swap participants
- designated contract markets and swap execution facilities
- national securities exchanges and security-based swap execution facilities
- market participants trading event contracts, structured notes, or perpetual-style products
- clearing entities, surveillance teams, and compliance staff
- institutional and retail market users who rely on clearer product classification

**Statutory Authority:** Title VII of the Dodd-Frank Act, CEA section 1a(47), Exchange Act section 3(a)(68), and the Commissions' joint authority to further define terms and coordinate where their regulatory interests overlap.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No rule text is proposed. The document asks whether new or revised rules, interpretations, registration pathways, reporting rules, or coordinated enforcement approaches would be appropriate.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice expressly says `ACTION: Joint request for comment.`
- The document devotes most of its questions to definitional line-drawing and to alternative compliance mechanics.
- The agency asks about products ranging from event contracts and binary options to notes, forwards, futures, and perpetual-style contracts.

**Inference:** This is an upstream boundary-setting inquiry. Public comments matter most if they connect a real product example to an administrable legal distinction or coordination model.

**Evidence Quotes**
- draw clearer regulatory lines
- potential approaches to enable alternative compliance
- substantially similar requirements
- best deter market manipulation
