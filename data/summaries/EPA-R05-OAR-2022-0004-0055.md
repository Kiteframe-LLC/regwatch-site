# Public Summary: EPA-R05-OAR-2022-0004-0055

## What This Document Does

### Observed Facts
- EPA proposes redesignating the Detroit area to attainment for the 2015 ozone NAAQS.
- EPA also proposes a 2040 maintenance plan, 2035 and 2040 mobile-source budgets, a reset of Moderate-classification deadlines, and final action on wildfire-related exceptional-events exclusions.

### Inference
- This is a bundled ozone redesignation action that links current attainment, future maintenance, transportation conformity, and implementation timing into one notice.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent overall, but the deadline reset and future-year budget logic would benefit from a more granular public record.
