# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3466-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal would amend the Table of TV Allotments in 47 CFR 73.622(j) for Colusa, California.
- It would substitute noncommercial educational UHF channel *14 for the station's current VHF channel *2.
- The petitioner is One Ministries, Inc., licensee of KEDS(TV).
- The stated rationale is improved viewer signal reception and full principal-community coverage by the proposed contour.

**Inference:** The practical policy choice is whether the expected local reception benefit justifies changing the allotment after the Commission confirms technical feasibility and considers any service, interference, or transition effects.

**Regulatory Action:** Revise the Table of TV Allotments for Colusa by replacing channel *2 with channel *14 for KEDS(TV).

**Affected Entities**
- KEDS(TV) and its licensee One Ministries, Inc.
- Colusa viewers, especially households using indoor antennas
- other viewers or stations potentially affected by contour or interference changes
- FCC Media Bureau and Office of the Secretary
- local communities and commenters evaluating television access

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies on the Commission's Communications Act authority cited for 47 CFR part 73, including 47 U.S.C. 154, 155, 301, 303, 307, 309, 310, 334, 336, and 339.

**Key Regulatory Change:** A single community-level digital television allotment would move from high-VHF channel *2 to UHF channel *14, subject to later licensing and technical compliance.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The Petitioner requests the substitution of UHF channel *14 in place of its current VHF channel *2 at Colusa in the Table.
- An engineering statement provided by the Petitioner confirms that the proposed channel *14 contour would provide full principal community coverage to Colusa.
