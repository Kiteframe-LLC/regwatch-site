# Public Summary: EPA-R05-OAR-2024-0618-0007

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve Illinois SIP revisions in 35 IAC parts 217, 218, and 219 as satisfying Moderate VOC and NO X RACT requirements and Serious NO X RACT requirements for the Chicago and Metro-East St. Louis 2015 ozone nonattainment areas. The proposal also approves negative declarations, a source-specific Pine Bend permit, and related testing, monitoring, and emissions-averaging provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- Illinois EPA and other permitting authorities
- Major VOC and NO X sources in the Chicago and Metro-East nonattainment areas
- The Flint Hills Resources Pine Bend facility
- Residents and communities affected by ozone pollution

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 172, 182(b)(2), 182(c), 182(f), and 182(g)(4), plus the EPA SIP Requirements Rules and RACT guidance cited in the notice.

**Key Regulatory Change:** If finalized, the action would incorporate the Illinois rules and specified permit/declarations into the Illinois SIP for the identified 2015 ozone obligations; Serious VOC RACT remains outside this action.
