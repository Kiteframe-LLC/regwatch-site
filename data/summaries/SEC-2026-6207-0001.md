# Public Summary: SEC-2026-6207-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The SEC proposes conditional adviser and regulated-fund self-custody, qualifying State trust company custody, crypto-network recordkeeping, and related custody, disclosure, and reporting amendments.

**Affected Entities:** Advisers, regulated funds and BDCs, boards, custodians, state trust companies, accountants, service providers, clients, shareholders, and small entities.

**Core Tradeoff:** Expanded access and flexibility may reduce custody friction, but the record must show equivalent safeguards when independent custody is unavailable.

**Observed Fact:** The notice contains economic analysis, alternatives, PRA, IRFA, and statutory authority.

**Inference:** The key record question is whether asset-specific controls, audits, network records, and correction duties adequately replace traditional custodian separation.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is broadly coherent, but the record needs stronger comparative evidence and more measurable safeguards before adviser self-custody, state trust company custody, and network-based recordkeeping can be shown to preserve equivalent protection.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Issue:** Add an explicit failure and correction pathway. **Why it matters:** Review may occur after an irreversible transfer or material loss risk. **Ask:** Specify adverse-event reporting, client notice, independent review, remediation deadlines, and a custodian-transition trigger. **Evidence:** I.A.1; II.A; II.C; II.H.
- **Issue:** Demonstrate equivalent protection before permitting self-custody. **Why it matters:** Self-custody changes the separation between advice and asset control. **Ask:** Publish comparative incident, recovery, audit, and control-performance evidence by asset and network. **Evidence:** I.A.2; II.A; IV.A-IV.C.
- **Issue:** Make custodian-availability and safeguarding-expertise determinations auditable. **Why it matters:** The gateway could be applied inconsistently. **Ask:** Add objective factors, a search record, independent challenge for high-risk assets, and a transition duty when a custodian becomes available. **Evidence:** II.A.2-II.A.3.
- **Issue:** Require a human-readable mirrored record and scale compliance for smaller entities. **Why it matters:** Network data can be hard to reconcile and control burdens vary by firm size. **Ask:** Add off-chain reconciliation and entity-size-specific estimates or phased compliance without reducing core safeguards. **Evidence:** II.H.3; IV.C; V. PRA; VI. IRFA.
