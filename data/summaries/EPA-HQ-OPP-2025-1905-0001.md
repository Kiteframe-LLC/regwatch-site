# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-1905-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA is seeking comment on a petition to establish a tolerance for cycloate residues on quinoa, as part of a notice that also summarizes other pesticide petition receipts.

**Affected Entities**
- petitioners and registrants seeking tolerance action
- growers and commodity groups tied to quinoa or related commodities
- food processors and handlers that rely on tolerance decisions
- laboratories and enforcement staff that would need to measure or verify residues
- public commenters concerned with residue, exposure, and enforcement consequences

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with related references to 40 CFR 180.7.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is styled as a notice of filing petitions and request for comment.
- EPA says it has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data yet.
- The notice discusses both tolerance changes and tolerance exemptions across multiple petitions.
- The docket contains a petitioner-authored summary for cycloate on quinoa and a related Federal Register notice that repeats the same docket number in a broader April 2026 applications notice.

**Inference:** The record is about record-building for multiple petition outcomes, not about finalizing a single uniform regulatory change.

**Evidence Quotes**

- may establish a final tolerance or tolerance exemption that may vary
- related commodities
- LC-MS/MS and GC-MS/MS methods are used to measure and evaluate the chemical cycloate
