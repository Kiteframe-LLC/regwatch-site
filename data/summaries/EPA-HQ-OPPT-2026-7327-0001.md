# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPPT-2026-7327-0001

## What This Document Does

Observed Fact: EPA proposes to extend selected TCE compliance dates to December 31, 2027, including specified processing, processing-aid, wastewater, and HFC-134a feedstock provisions, and to update downstream notification/SDS language 30 days after final publication. Affected entities include chemical processors, specified industrial and commercial users, HFC-134a manufacturers, and TCE distributors. Authority: TSCA sections 6(a) and 6(d). Inference: this is a targeted implementation correction, but the record should better document the operational constraints and the incremental effects of the selected dates.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent as a targeted date extension, with ordinary weaknesses in incremental quantification and use-specific tailoring.
