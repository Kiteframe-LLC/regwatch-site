# Public Summary: FCC-2026-2839-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The FCC is proposing further routine conditions for submarine cable landing licensees and SLTE owners/operators.
- The proposal asks whether existing licensees should carry downstream contractual obligations to ensure SLTE compliance.
- The notice also asks whether routine conditions should vary by ownership, leasing, or hybrid arrangements.
- The notice seeks comment on adopting national-security standards such as NIST CSF, CISA CPGs, and CIS Controls.
- The notice asks whether existing licensees should be allowed to opt in to ten standards instead of maintaining existing NSAs.

**Affected Entities**
- Cable landing licensees subject to the current licensing requirement
- SLTE owners and operators that are not separately licensed under the current sections identified in the notice
- Licensees with downstream entities that own, operate, lease, or resell SLTE-related capacity or spectrum
- Small entities that may need new contract, reporting, or cybersecurity compliance systems
- Consumers and network users whose service continuity depends on submarine cable reliability

**Statutory Authority:** Sections 1, 4(i), 4(j), 201-255, 303(r), 403, and 413 of the Communications Act, plus the Cable Landing License Act.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The notice would extend the submarine-cable compliance conversation from direct licensing conditions to routine conditions, contractual enforcement, and optional national-security standards.

**Inference**
- This is a national-security-driven attempt to make SLTE oversight more uniform across cable landing licensees and related downstream operators.
- The comment leverage is centered on whether the proposed compliance chain is precise enough to administer and verify without unnecessary burden.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The Commission seeks comment on routine conditions for SLTE oversight.
- Should the Commission require cable landing licensees to ensure downstream adherence?
- Should the Commission adopt national-security standards such as NIST CSF or CISA CPGs?
- What, if any, would be the impact on small entities?
