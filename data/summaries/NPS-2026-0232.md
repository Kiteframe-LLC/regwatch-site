# Public Summary: NPS-2026-0232-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NPS proposes a new 36 CFR 13.1005 that would permit off-road vehicles for subsistence use by local rural residents of Anaktuvuk Pass in Gates of the Arctic National Park and Preserve, while allowing the Superintendent to impose evidence-based mitigation measures, including closures, under specified procedures.

**Affected Entities**
- Local rural residents of Anaktuvuk Pass engaged in subsistence uses
- Nunamiut and other Alaska Native community members and associated entities
- National Park Service personnel and the Gates Superintendent
- Subsistence resources, wildlife, tundra, historic and scientific values
- Local businesses or service providers potentially affected indirectly

**Statutory Authority:** ANILCA sections 201(4)(a) and 811, 16 U.S.C. 3121, together with the NPS authority cited for 36 CFR part 13.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The rule would codify that ORVs are a traditionally employed means of surface transportation for authorized residents, establish operating standards, and create notice, consultation, written-determination, least-restrictive-measure, emergency, and continuing-access requirements for later mitigation.

**Observed Facts**
- The proposed definition of authorized residents is local rural residents of Anaktuvuk Pass.
- The adverse-impact definition covers direct significant impacts to health and safety, resources, historic or scientific values, subsistence uses, and listed species.
- Emergency mitigation may be imposed without advance notice or consultation, with Regional Director concurrence, and expires after 45 days.

**Inference:** The proposal is access-protective in its stated default but leaves the practical balance between access and resource protection dependent on future Superintendent findings under terms that are not fully operationalized in the notice.

**Evidence Quotes**
- off-road vehicles are permitted to be used by authorized residents for subsistence purposes
- the Superintendent may require the implementation of reasonable mitigation measures (including closures)
- must be supported by specific, articulable facts in the administrative record
