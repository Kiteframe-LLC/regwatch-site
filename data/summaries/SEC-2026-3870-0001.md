# Public Summary: SEC-2026-3870-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Joint RFI asking whether the SEC and CFTC should clarify lines among swaps, security-based swaps, mixed swaps, and excluded instruments, and whether alternative compliance should be allowed where requirements are substantially similar.

**Affected Entities**
- swap dealers and major swap participants
- security-based swap dealers and major security-based swap participants
- exchanges, swap execution facilities, and security-based swap execution facilities
- market participants offering or trading event contracts, structured notes, options, forwards, futures, or perpetual-style products
- clearing, compliance, surveillance, and enforcement personnel
- institutional and retail market users

**Statutory Authority:** Title VII of the Dodd-Frank Act, including CEA section 1a(47), Exchange Act section 3(a)(68), and the Commissions' authority to further define Title VII terms.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No rule text is proposed. The notice seeks evidence for possible future rules, interpretations, registration pathways, reporting arrangements, or coordinated compliance mechanisms.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice states ACTION: Joint request for comment.
- The document asks about event contracts, options on securities, narrow- and broad-based indexes, single-security and event-contract prongs, structured notes, forwards, futures, and alternative compliance.
- The Commissions invite data-driven comments on all aspects of the questions.

**Inference:** This is an upstream boundary-setting inquiry. The highest-value comments will connect a real product or compliance requirement to a specific, administrable distinction.
