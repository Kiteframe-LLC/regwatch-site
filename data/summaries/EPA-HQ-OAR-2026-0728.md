# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2026-0728-0114

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to revise compliance and test procedures for MY 2027+ heavy-duty highway engines, adjust useful-life and warranty periods, expand NCP availability for Medium HDE and Heavy HDE, and revise SCR inducement provisions with possible in-use guidance.

**Affected Entities**
- heavy-duty engine manufacturers
- truck and vehicle manufacturers
- component suppliers and dealerships
- fleets and operators that depend on DEF/SCR compliance
- nonroad diesel engine and equipment manufacturers
- state, local, and federal compliance stakeholders

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act (CAA) (42 U.S.C. 7401-7671q)

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is an NPRM with a full set of requested sections, including costs, emissions, air quality, health, and executive-order review.
- The proposal explicitly revisits useful-life/warranty settings while also changing NCP and inducement policy.

**Inference:** The rule is a technical implementation rule with real compliance and operational effects; the highest-value comments will focus on calibration, evidence, and how the changes fit together.

**Evidence Quotes**
- make nonconformance penalties (NCPs) available
- changes to the regulatory useful life periods
- amend the requirements for selective catalytic reduction (SCR) system inducement provisions
- considering new inducement guidance for in-use highway and nonroad diesel engines
