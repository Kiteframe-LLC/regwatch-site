# Public Summary: OPM-2025-0012-0678

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** OPM and MSPB propose faster performance and adverse-action procedures, expanded supervisor training, and a new MSPB penalty-review standard. The reopening notice adds current action data and a nongovernmental report.

**Affected Entities**
- Federal employees subject to performance-based or adverse action procedures
- Supervisors, managers, and executives responsible for performance management
- Executive-branch agencies and their human-resources, labor-relations, and legal offices
- MSPB judges, the Board, employee representatives, and unions involved in review or bargaining

**Statutory Authority:** The proposal cites OPM and MSPB personnel authorities and the statutory efficiency-of-the-service standard.

**Key Regulatory Changes**
- Set a generally 30-day performance opportunity with agency extensions.
- Limit extra assistance periods and make removal the default penalty.
- Replace routine Douglas-factor review with totality review.
- Expand supervisor training and restrict record-erasing settlements.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice reports specified FY 2019-2025 counts and partial FY 2026 counts, including a separate ZLM category.
- It says FY 2026 totals are broadly consistent with FY 2025 and acknowledges possible confounders.
- The proposal contains the 30-day period and totality-review changes.

**Inference:** The package seeks faster, more standardized accountability, but the new data do not isolate the cause of observed action levels.

**Evidence Quotes**
- reopened for two weeks
- consistent with Fiscal Year 2025 levels
- Data through June 2026
- maximum of 30 calendar days
