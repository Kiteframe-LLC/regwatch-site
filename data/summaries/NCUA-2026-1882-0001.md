# Public Summary: NCUA-2026-1882-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NCUA proposes to remove prescriptive bond procedures, replace specific coverage guidance with annual risk review, and remove ten-year form expiration.

**Affected Entities:** Federally insured and corporate credit unions, boards and committees, eligible signatories, and fidelity-bond carriers.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites Federal Credit Union Act authority for bonding rules and NCUA approval of bond forms.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal permits delegated review, removes signatory rotation and form expiration, removes § 713.5(b)-(c), and asks about § 704.18(d)(2).

**Observed Facts**
- The notice traces the framework through 1999, 2005, 2019, and 2021 amendments.
- It identifies fraud-related revocation and outdated-form concerns from 2019.
- The text retains annual review, non-employee signing, and NCUA form approval.

**Inference**
- The main gap is the absence of quantified burden, risk, and monitoring evidence.
- The strongest comments request operational safeguards, evidence, and workable alternatives.

**Evidence Quotes**
- **Section:** Summary
  
  **Quote:** Reduced administrative costs without compromising safety and soundness.
- **Section:** II.A
  
  **Quote:** The automatic sunset creates an unnecessary recurring burden.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is generally coherent, but its qualitative safety and burden claims are not matched by quantified evidence or a clear monitoring plan for the safeguards being removed.
