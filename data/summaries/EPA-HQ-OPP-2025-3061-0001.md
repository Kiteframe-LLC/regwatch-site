## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Notice of receipt and request for comment on pesticide petitions. For the selected docket, PP 5F9204 requests amended indoxacarb tolerances in milk, cattle fat, meat, and byproducts. The notice does not establish or amend a tolerance.

**Affected Entities**
- pesticide registrants and petitioners
- dairy and cattle producers
- food and feed processors and handlers
- consumers and public-health commenters
- farm workers and communities potentially exposed to pesticide residues
- EPA and other residue-monitoring authorities

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with petition-data requirements described in section 408(d)(2) and 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No immediate regulatory change. EPA is collecting comments before evaluating whether and what tolerance action is warranted.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice lists requested ppm levels for milk and cattle commodities.
- The notice states EPA has not fully evaluated data sufficiency.
- The notice says additional data may be needed and that a final tolerance may vary from the petition request.

**Inference:** The highest-value comments are evidence requests and commodity-specific challenges to the residue, analytical, and related-commodity record, rather than claims that the notice itself already sets an unlawful tolerance.
