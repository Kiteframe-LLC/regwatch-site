# Public Summary: CMS-2026-3235-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** CMS and CDC propose CLIA amendments covering virtual access under a primary laboratory certificate, digital-image gynecologic cytology PT, personnel qualifications, and reporting, enforcement, consultation, editorial, and technical updates.

**Affected Entities:** Clinical laboratories, directors, pathologists, cytotechnologists, PT programs, accreditation organizations, exempt States, providers, and patients.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites 42 U.S.C. 263a, 1302, 1395x(e), and 1395x(s)(3) and (s)(17), implementing PHSA section 353.

**Observed Facts**
- Virtual access covers digital data, results, live imaging, and images through secure connections, excluding cytology.
- Digital-image cytology PT and revised qualification pathways are proposed with integrity and oversight provisions.
- The RIA reports wide cost ranges and leaves several virtual-access costs unquantified.

**Inference:** The package is coherent as CLIA modernization, but final support depends on measurable quality, access, burden, and implementation evidence.

**Evidence Quotes**
- **Section:** Summary
  
  **Quote:** The rule would allow virtual access under the primary testing laboratory's CLIA certificate.
- **Section:** VI.B. Overall Impact
  
  **Quote:** Initial costs range from $19,352,721 to $40,654,504.
