# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2245-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** CMS proposes to update ESRD payment rates, facility adjustments, AKI dialysis payment, ESRD QIP measures, and dialysis-care RFIs for CY 2027 and PY 2029.

**Affected Entities**
- ESRD facilities, including hospital-based and freestanding providers
- Patients receiving dialysis, including beneficiaries with AKI
- Pediatric ESRD patients and facilities serving small or low-volume populations
- CMS contractors and the Medicare program

**Observed Facts**
- The notice states ACTION: Proposed rule and identifies CY 2027 ESRD PPS and AKI payment changes.
- The notice also proposes ESRD QIP measure revisions and requests comment on additional dialysis-care topics.

**Inference:** This is a detailed payment-and-quality rule rather than a narrow procedural notice, so commenters have real leverage on calibration, measurement, and transition design.

**Evidence Quotes**
- This proposed rule would update and revise the End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) Prospective Payment System for calendar year 2027.
- These RFIs solicit comments on increasing home dialysis uptake, improving palliative dialysis care, and supporting alternative dialysis schedules.

## Why It Matters

  **Quote:** We considered, but did not propose, delaying the incorporation of phosphate binders and continuing TDAPA for another year.
- **Section:** VIII.C.5

- **Section:** II.B.11

  **Quote:** We are proposing to expand the LVPA to ESRD facilities which furnish up to 8,000 treatments per year.
- **Section:** VIII.C.5.a
  
  **Quote:** Hospital-based ESRD facilities would have an estimated 2.0 percent increase in Medicare payments compared with freestanding ESRD facilities with an estimated 1.1 percent increase.

  **Quote:** approximately 459 ESRD facilities, at the firm level, can be considered small according to the SBA
- **Section:** II.B.11
