# Public Summary: CPSC-2023-0004-0116

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Exploratory notice asking for public input on whether CPSC should accept or reject, in whole or in part, UL 4200A-2026 as a revision to the voluntary standard incorporated into the button-cell and coin-battery safety rule.

**Affected Entities**
- children and families exposed to button-cell or coin-battery ingestion hazards
- manufacturers, importers, retailers, and testing laboratories for covered consumer products
- UL and other standards participants
- consumers and organizations seeking safe products and transparent technical standards
- small businesses and public-interest commenters with limited access to proprietary standards

**Observed Facts**
- The notice asks whether UL 4200A-2026 improves safety.
- The existing mandatory rule incorporates UL 4200A-2023 with additional requirements.
- The notice does not state the specific revisions in UL 4200A-2026 or propose a regulatory amendment.

**Inference:** The most useful comments will supply a concrete comparison of the editions, identify safety consequences of each change, and test whether the public had a meaningful opportunity to evaluate the incorporated material.

**Evidence Quotes**
- whether the revision improves the safety
- UL 4200A-2026
- the short statutory time frame
- CPSC will not consider comments received after this date
