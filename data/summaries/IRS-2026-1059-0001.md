# Public Summary: IRS-2026-1059-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The proposal updates 26 CFR 1.430(d)-1 under Code sections 430 and 7805.
- It addresses investment-related expenses, late plans and amendments, remedial operations, a two-times anti-abuse test, pending actuarial approvals, and mortality-related conforming edits.
- The proposed applicability date is six months after final-rule publication.

**Inference:** This is a technical implementation rule, but its classifications and timing tests can affect actuarial calculations and deductible contribution timing.

**Regulatory Action:** Update minimum-funding regulations for single-employer defined benefit plans.

**Affected Entities:** Employers, plan administrators, enrolled actuaries, participants and beneficiaries, service providers, small sponsors, and professional advisers.

**Statutory Authority:** Code sections 430(g)(3)(B), 430(h)(3), and 7805, with related sections 401(b), 404(o), 412, and 436.

**Key Changes:** Define expense categories and a $5,000 itemization rule; specify late-adoption and remedial-amendment treatment; add the disproportionate-increase test; and update actuarial-assumption and mortality rules.

**Evidence Quotes:** The summary says the proposal updates target normal cost and funding target rules; the authority section identifies sections 430 and 7805; and the applicability section provides a six-month-after-finalization date.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Issue:** The small-entity and no-meaningful-behavior-change conclusions are not quantified. **Why it matters:** Small sponsors may face concentrated implementation costs. **Ask:** Explain the data, population assumptions, and transition-cost analysis. **Posture:** evidence request.
- **Issue:** The two-times ratio may behave inconsistently across plan designs. **Why it matters:** It determines when a late amendment enters current-year calculations. **Ask:** Publish examples, explain denominator treatment, and compare the ratio with the alternative mentioned in the notice. **Posture:** alternatives push.
- **Issue:** Bundled service-provider payments may not be readily itemized. **Why it matters:** Similar plans could classify expenses differently. **Ask:** Add safe harbors or allocation examples for bundled contracts and partial-year changes. **Posture:** collaborative clarification.
- **Issue:** The correction path for late elections or approvals is incomplete. **Why it matters:** Administrators need to know whether reports, contributions, or filings must be revised. **Ask:** Provide timelines and correction examples. **Posture:** procedural concern.
