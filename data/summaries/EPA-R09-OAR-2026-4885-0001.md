# Public Summary: EPA-R09-OAR-2026-4885-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- EPA proposes a clean data determination for the Sacramento Metro area under the 1997 8-hour ozone NAAQS, based on the 2023-2025 design-value test.
- Finalization would suspend specified attainment-related planning requirements while leaving the area nonattainment and retaining emissions inventory, nonattainment NSR, and applicable section 185 fee requirements.
- The notice identifies CARB, five regional air districts, and three Tribal jurisdictions within the area.

**Inference**
- This is a status action, not a new emissions-control rule. The main public-interest question is whether EPA explains the data exceptions and durability of the attainment showing clearly enough to support the planning relief.

**Regulatory Action:** Determine attainment and issue a clean data determination suspending specified attainment-related planning obligations while the area continues to attain.

**Affected Entities**
- Residents and communities in the Sacramento Metro area
- CARB and the five regional air districts
- Three Tribal jurisdictions within the area
- Sources, permit holders, and stakeholders relying on continued monitoring and nonattainment accountability

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites the CAA, 40 CFR 50.10, part 50 appendix I, 40 CFR 51.918, and part 58.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Suspend specified attainment-related SIP planning submissions without redesignation or suspension of remaining nonattainment requirements.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is proposing to issue a CDD for the Sacramento Metro, California nonattainment area to reflect our finding that the area is attaining the 1997 8-hour ozone NAAQS.
