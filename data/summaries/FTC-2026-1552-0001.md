# Public Summary: FTC-2026-1552-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FTC is seeking information before deciding whether to amend the existing Government and Business Impersonation Rule, promulgate a new rule, or pursue nonregulatory measures addressing platform practices that further government and business impersonation scams.

**Document Classification:** Advance notice of proposed rulemaking (ANPRM); request for public comment.

**Objective:** Build an administrative record on whether platform advertising and optimization practices constitute or further unfair or deceptive acts and practices, whether those practices are prevalent, and what intervention would be effective and proportionate.

**Covered Conduct Under Consideration**
- advertisement content creation or modification
- audience targeting and delivery optimization
- search-query or consumer-profile tailoring
- advertiser identity verification
- screening, monitoring, investigation, takedown, and discipline
- complaint handling and recordkeeping
- possibly non-advertising posts and other platform functions

**Possible Measures**
- amending the existing Impersonation Rule
- adopting a new rule or means-and-instrumentalities provision
- affirmative prevention and response duties
- a safe harbor tied to specified practices
- recordkeeping and complaint procedures
- consumer/business education or voluntary industry coordination

**Affected Entities**
- consumers, including potentially vulnerable consumers
- legitimate businesses and small-business advertisers
- search engines, social-media services, and digital marketplaces
- government entities and businesses whose identities are impersonated
- advertisers and advertising-service providers
- consumer advocates, researchers, and enforcement agencies

**Core Tradeoff:** Reducing scam harm may require platform screening and intervention, but can impose cost, false-positive, privacy, competition, innovation, and speech burdens.

**Observed Fact:** The notice asks commenters to identify both helpful and unhelpful requirements and to describe consumer, platform, legitimate-business, and small-business costs.

**Inference:** The eventual rule's defensibility will depend heavily on a precise conduct boundary and a documented comparison of remedies.

**Unknown:** No final regulatory text or selected alternative is available in the supplied record.
