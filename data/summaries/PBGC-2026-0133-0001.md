# Public Summary: PBGC-2026-0133-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- PBGC proposes to codify prior FAQ guidance on permissible investments and derivative exposure.
- PBGC also proposes to fix the withdrawal-liability valuation date and remove the health-benefit reallocation exception.
- The notice includes the usual NPRM analysis sections, including Executive Order, RFA, and PRA discussion.

**Inference**
- This is a targeted NPRM that translates existing guidance into text, tightens one approval gate, and removes one exception path.
- The agency appears to think the program can be clarified and streamlined without changing the basic SFA framework.

**Evidence**
- The proposal is framed as technical corrections, clarifications, updates, and improvements.
- The proposed text fixes the withdrawal-liability valuation date and repeals the exception procedure.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Id:** angle_short_period
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** The proposal needs a tighter rule for how long the derivative bridge may last.
  
  **Why It Matters:** A vague time limit can force plans to over-disclose, over-restrict, or guess at compliance.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Give a hard outer limit or at least worked examples that distinguish a bridge position from a policy hedge.
  
  **Supporting Evidence:** The notice says the relevant facts and circumstances determine the appropriate length of time.
  
  **Score Drivers**
  - pass4.arbitrary_line_drawing_risk
  - pass5.ambiguity_enabled_gatekeeping
- **Id:** angle_related_settlements
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** The related-settlement aggregation rule should be defined with examples.
  
  **Why It Matters:** Without examples, the approval gate can turn on subjective judgments about relatedness and anti-avoidance purpose.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Explain how the rule applies to related employers, partial withdrawals, and transactions spread over time.
  
  **Supporting Evidence:** The proposal aggregates settlements arising from related withdrawals, transactions, or arrangements and adds an anti-avoidance clause.
  
  **Score Drivers**
  - pass4.enforcement_clarity_risk
  - pass5.procedural_gateway_control
- **Id:** angle_valuation_date
  
  **Priority:** high
  
  **Issue:** PBGC should justify the prior-year valuation date or allow a more current fallback when volatility is material.
  
  **Why It Matters:** A stale valuation date can change whether PBGC approval is required and can affect settlement timing.
  
  **Ask Agency To:** Explain why the prior-year date is the best proxy and whether a volatility-based exception is needed.
  
  **Supporting Evidence:** The rule ties the threshold to the present value of withdrawal-liability payments discounted as of the prior plan year-end.
  
  **Score Drivers**
