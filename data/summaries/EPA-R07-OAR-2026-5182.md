# Public Summary: EPA-R07-OAR-2026-5182-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- EPA proposes to approve Missouri SIP revisions to 10 CSR 10-6.061, Construction Permit Exemptions.
- The revisions simplify language, update references, refine emergency-generator exemptions, and revise reporting and recordkeeping requirements.
- The TSD explains the emergency-generator aggregation change, the SMAL citation correction, and the revised recordkeeping language.

**Inference**
- This is primarily a clarifying SIP approval rather than a major policy rewrite.
- The strongest comment leverage appears to be implementation clarity for emergency-generator projects and the revised compliance record trail.

**Evidence Quotes**
- This amendment simplifies language and restructures for clarity.
- This amendment also updates references to other rules, incorporations by reference, and reporting and recordkeeping requirements.
- The revisions to Subparagraph (3)(A)2.BB. above add additional conditions to this exemption and also clarify eligibility for this exemption for emergency generators.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of construction or modification projects that rely on 10 CSR 10-6.061 exemptions
- Owners and operators of emergency-generator installations in Missouri
- Missouri Department of Natural Resources
- EPA Region 7 permit reviewers and inspectors

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is mostly coherent, but it tightens an emergency-generator exemption while also rewriting recordkeeping and citation language that outside readers may need help following.
