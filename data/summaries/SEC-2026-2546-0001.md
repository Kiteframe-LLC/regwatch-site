# Public Summary: SEC-2026-2546-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Joint SEC/CFTC proposal to amend Form PF by raising filing and reporting thresholds, simplifying some reporting fields, and deleting several current or monthly reporting items.

**Affected Entities:** SEC-registered private-fund advisers, including advisers that also register with the CFTC as CPOs or CTAs; large hedge fund advisers; private equity fund advisers; smaller advisers that may fall below the new thresholds.

**Statutory Authority:** Advisers Act and Form PF authority, plus the usual PRA, RFA, and CRA procedural framework.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Raise the all-filer threshold from $150 million to $1 billion, raise the large hedge fund adviser threshold from $1.5 billion to $10 billion, and remove or simplify several detailed reporting requirements.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice has a full proposed-rule preamble and a detailed economic analysis.
- The notice presents threshold estimates, cost-savings estimates, and reasonable alternatives.
- The notice includes a 12-month transition period and a private-credit request for comment.

**Inference:** This is a true NPRM with a substantial analytic spine, and the main comment question is whether the reporting reductions preserve enough useful supervisory information.
