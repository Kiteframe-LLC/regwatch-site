# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2311-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Update CY 2027 home-health payment and case-mix formulas; revise HH QRP reporting; clarify DMEPOS replacement and infusion-pump coverage; expand provider-enrollment authorities; and seek information on a home-health wage index and future measures.

**Affected Entities**
- Home health agencies
- Medicare beneficiaries and caregivers
- Clinicians and DMEPOS suppliers
- Accreditation organizations
- Medicare contractors and CMS

**Core Tradeoff:** More current payment and program-integrity controls versus payment volatility, administrative burden, access risk, and due-process safeguards.

**Observed Facts**
- The rule reports 7,719,986 30-day periods and 2,501,044 unique beneficiaries in CY 2025.
- It reports a CY 2025 payment rate of $2,057.35 against estimated average 30-day costs of $1,532.84.
- It proposes a temporary behavior adjustment, PDGM recalibration, and enrollment sanctions.

**Inference:** CMS is combining routine annual updates with consequential payment recovery and oversight changes, while leaving some palliative-care and wage-index design questions open.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The regulatory architecture is coherent, but payment recovery and enrollment controls create material risk if evidence and review safeguards are incomplete.
