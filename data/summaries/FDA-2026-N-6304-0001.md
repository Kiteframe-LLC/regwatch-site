# Public Summary: FDA-2026-N-6304-0001

## What This Document Does

**Affected Entities**
- Food manufacturers, packers, and importers using or holding Citrus Red No. 2
- Orange growers and handlers whose products may rely on the listing
- FDA and public-health stakeholders concerned with color-additive oversight

**Observed Facts**
- FDA proposes to remove 21 CFR 74.302, which lists Citrus Red No. 2 for coloring the skins of mature oranges.
- The notice cites FD&C Act sections 701 and 721 and certification history through 2020.
- The proposal includes a 90-day effective date and asks about a one-year compliance period.

**Inference:** FDA is treating the listing as obsolete because authorized use appears to have ended, while preserving time for any remaining certified supply to be depleted.

**Evidence Quotes**
- “we tentatively conclude that the authorized use of Citrus Red No. 2 has been abandoned.”
- “We request comments on whether to provide such a compliance period and the appropriate duration.”
