# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2080-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** CMS proposes to codify drug-negotiation policies and related Part D rules, including several edge-case implementation provisions.

**Affected Entities**
- beneficiaries
- manufacturers
- acquiring entities
- generic and biosimilar firms
- CMS staff

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says CMS is codifying policies established in final guidance, with limited modification, into regulatory text.
- The notice lists multiple operational proposals: fixed-combination new formulations, orphan-drug timing, bona fide marketing review, transfer of responsibility, one-time supply calculations, a temporary floor for small biotech drugs, and off-label use treatment.
- The notice says the final requirements will apply beginning with initial price applicability year 2029.

**Inference:** The strongest comments will focus on whether the edge-case mechanics are specific enough to work without constant new guidance.

**Evidence Quotes**
- We propose to codify policies established in final guidance for the Negotiation Program in regulatory text.
- Proposed 429.125(b)(4)(i) would clarify treatment of new formulations.
- Proposed 429.415(a)(2) would explain how CMS would calculate the 30-day equivalent supply for a selected drug that is typically administered one time.
- Proposed 429.440 would explain how CMS would implement the Temporary Floor for Small Biotech Drugs for initial price applicability years 2029 and 2030.
