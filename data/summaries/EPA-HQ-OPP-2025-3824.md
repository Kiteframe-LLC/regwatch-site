# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-3824-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Notice of filing and request for comment on pesticide petition applications that seek tolerance determinations for fenpropathrin and abamectin in or on all food/feed items when the ingredients are used in food/feed handling establishments or as wide-area mosquito adulticides.

**Affected Entities**
- food and feed handlers
- pesticide registrants and petitioners
- growers and commodity groups that could be affected by residue thresholds
- public health and environmental commenters concerned about wide-area mosquito adulticide uses
- EPA staff responsible for residue chemistry, enforcement methods, and tolerance decisions

**Observed Facts**
- The notice invites public comment and says EPA has not fully evaluated the data yet.
- The docket already includes petitioner-prepared summaries for the abamectin and fenpropathrin requests.
- The requested tolerances are stated in ppm and tied to two broad use patterns.

**Inference:** The most useful public comments will likely address whether the residue and analytical-method record is robust enough for the requested tolerances, especially given the broad food/feed and mosquito-adulticide use scenarios.

**Evidence Quotes**
- notice of the receipt of pesticide petitions
- public comments on the requests before responding
- not fully evaluated the sufficiency
- wide-area mosquito adulticide
- LC-MS/MS method
