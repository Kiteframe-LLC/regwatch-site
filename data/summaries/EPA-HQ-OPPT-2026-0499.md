## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a proposed rule with a public comment deadline of July 10, 2026.
- The preamble says EPA evaluated SNUN costs and export-notification costs and points readers to docketed support materials.

  **Why it matters:** Commenters need to know whether the final rule is faithful to the Orders or adds new substance-specific limits.
