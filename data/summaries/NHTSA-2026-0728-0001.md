# Public Summary: NHTSA-2026-0728-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NHTSA proposes to amend FMVSS No. 135 so ADS-equipped vehicles without manually operated driving controls can comply through on-board brake actuation and updated definitions, while keeping the existing stopping-distance performance requirements in place.

**Affected Entities**
- Manufacturers of ADS-equipped light vehicles without manually operated driving controls
- Manufacturers that convert conventional vehicles into ADS-equipped platforms
- Compliance and certification teams that must interpret placard, brake-control, and test-procedure wording
- Fleet operators and inspectors that rely on consistent brake-control and stopping-performance rules

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the proposal would distinguish how the regulations apply to vehicles with and without manually operated driving controls.
- The notice says the existing stopping-distance requirements would still apply to all subject vehicles.
- The notice says some current manual-control requirements would not apply to vehicles designed never to be operated by a human.
- The notice includes alternative test procedures for vehicles without manually operated driving controls.

**Inference**
- This is a modernization and scoping amendment rather than a rewrite of the standard's safety performance core.
- The agency's main judgment is that manual brake controls are not inherently necessary for ADS-only vehicles if on-board systems can satisfy the same performance goal.

**Evidence Quotes**
- This rulemaking would remove unnecessary regulatory burdens and costs without detriment to vehicle safety.
- For vehicles without manually operated driving controls, the service brake control and the parking brake control are activated by on-board systems.
- NHTSA is not proposing changes to any stopping distance requirements in FMVSS No. 135.
