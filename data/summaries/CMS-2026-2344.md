# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2344-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** CMS proposes annual OPPS and ASC payment updates, a new 340B drug payment formula and non-drug offset increase, a prior authorization expansion for botulinum toxin injections, an off-campus PBD payment-control method for imaging without contrast, ASC covered procedure and IPO list changes, quality-reporting refinements, and RFIs on Hospital Price Transparency and IPPS PPE/essential-medicine payment.

**Affected Entities**
- Hospitals and CMHCs paid under the OPPS
- Ambulatory Surgical Centers
- Hospitals subject to the 340B remedy offset
- Off-campus provider-based departments and rural Sole Community Hospitals
- Hospitals and ASCs participating in quality reporting programs
- Hospital Price Transparency stakeholders and hospital accrediting organizations

**Observed Facts**
- The notice gives a 2.4 percent OPPS and ASC update factor, a 1.9 percent overall OPPS payment increase estimate, and a 3 percent 340B-related conversion-factor reduction for affected hospitals.
- The notice adds 618 codes to the ASC covered procedures list, removes 637 services from the IPO list, and expands prior authorization to additional botulinum toxin injection services.

**Inference**
- The rule is a broad annual payment and program update rather than a single-policy rule, so the strongest comments will focus on the largest payment redistributions and the most operationally sensitive controls.
- The record is strongest on payment estimates and burden estimates, and weaker on downstream access, comparability, and replacement-signal questions.

**Evidence Quotes**
- We estimate that the proposed policies in this proposed rule will result in a 1.9 percent increase in OPPS payments to providers for services.
- We propose to expand the prior authorization requirement to include additional Botulinum Toxin Injection services.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent overall, but the 340B payment bridge and the prior-authorization expansion need stronger explanation of how the chosen mechanisms map to outcomes and access protections.
