# Public Summary: NHTSA-2025-0051-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** NHTSA proposes to amend FMVSS No. 225 and related reporting provisions to extend some compliance dates, grant some reconsideration requests, deny others, and make technical corrections.

**Affected Entities**
- Vehicle manufacturers
- Small-volume manufacturers
- Convertible vehicle manufacturers
- Child restraint system manufacturers and suppliers
- Vehicle compliance and certification staff
- Families and child passengers using child restraint systems

**Observed Facts**
- The notice responds to petitions for reconsideration of the January 7, 2025 final rule.
- The proposal would extend the phase-in schedule, extend the small-volume-manufacturer compliance date, and extend the convertible tether-anchor exemption sunset date.
- The proposal would deny the request to allow tether routing over adjustable or removable head restraints.
- The proposal would also make technical corrections and clarify certain test procedures and reporting requirements.

**Inference:** This is a detailed NPRM with a real implementation layer: the core policy choice is clear, but the burden, alternatives, and compliance-clarity record is where comments can still improve the proposal.

**Evidence Quotes**
- NHTSA proposes extending the 3-year phase-in period to a 4-year phase-in period.
- NHTSA proposes granting Alliance's request to extend the lead time for SVMs.
- NHTSA proposes denying Mercedes-Benz's requests to allow tether routing over adjustable or removable head restraints.
- The agency is also proposing to make several technical corrections, and clarify certain test procedures.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal extends compliance timelines and adjusts child-restraint anchorage requirements, but the notice only partially quantifies the burden and safety tradeoffs.
