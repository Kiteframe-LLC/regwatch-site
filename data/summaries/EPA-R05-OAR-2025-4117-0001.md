# Public Summary: EPA-R05-OAR-2025-4117-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to redesignate the Berrien and Muskegon areas to attainment, approve the maintenance plans and conformity budgets, approve the enhanced monitoring plan, approve serious VOC RACT for the remaining non-CTG sources, and approve a section 182(f) NOx waiver.

**Affected Entities:** Local counties, state air agencies, transportation planning organizations, stationary sources in the two areas, and the public that can comment on the SIP package.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies on CAA sections 107(d)(3)(E), 110(k), 175A, 182(c), and 182(f), plus the related SIP and conformity provisions.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would move the two areas from serious nonattainment treatment to attainment while preserving control obligations through maintenance plans, budgets, permit incorporations, and the NOx waiver.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the 2015 ozone design value for Muskegon is 0.070 ppm.
- The addendum says Berrien and Muskegon are in attainment based on the 2023-2025 design value and supporting exceptional-events materials.
- The addendum also says the May 2026 submittal resolves the remaining serious VOC and NOx planning requirements for the two areas.

**Inference**
- The proposal is a package deal: redesignation, maintenance, conformity, monitoring, VOC RACT, and NOx waiver all move together.
- The main policy dispute is likely to be whether the threshold screens and transport rationale are explained clearly enough for the public record.
