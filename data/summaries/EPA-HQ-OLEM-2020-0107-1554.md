# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OLEM-2020-0107-1554

## What This Document Does

**What It Does:** Extends comments on EPA's Legacy/CCRMU Amendments NPRM. The underlying proposal would revise CCR rules for dewatering structures, legacy units, CCR management units, site-specific monitoring/cleanup/closure decisions, and beneficial use.

**Why It Matters:** The proposal could make key CCR protections more site-specific and change what counts as regulated disposal versus exempt handling or beneficial use.

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of electric utility CCR units, legacy impoundments, and CCR management units
- State and federal permit writers
- Communities near CCR units and groundwater pathways
- Entities handling or reusing CCR in non-roadway applications
- Commenters focused on groundwater protection, closure integrity, beneficial use, and environmental justice

**Observed Facts**
- Comments now run to June 29, 2026.
- The underlying NPRM is the April 13, 2026 Legacy/CCRMU Amendments proposal.

**Inference:** Useful comments should test whether EPA has clear minimum protections and reproducible criteria for each flexibility provision.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA is extending the comment period until June 29, 2026.
- EPA is proposing to establish a new compliance pathway that allows for site-specific considerations during permitting.
