# Public Summary: EPA-R03-OAR-2026-4225-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory action:** EPA proposes to approve Maryland's negative declaration for existing OSWI and amend 40 CFR part 62 so no Maryland state plan is required while no covered source exists.

**Affected entities:** MDE, EPA Region III, possible future Maryland OSWI operators and applicants, air-permitting staff, and communities concerned about incinerator emissions.

**Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 111(d) and 129; 40 CFR 60.23(b), 60.23a(b), 62.06, and subpart FFFF.

**Observed Fact:** MDE says it reviewed state permits and emissions inventories and found no covered operating source.

**Inference:** The legal route is clear, but the supplied record does not expose the screening crosswalk or specify the interim path for a future borderline source.

**Core tradeoff:** Avoid an unnecessary state plan while keeping future source identification and coverage auditable.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is legally conventional and structurally coherent, but its screening basis and future-source safeguard are only lightly operationalized.
