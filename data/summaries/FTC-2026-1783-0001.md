# Public Summary: FTC-2026-1783-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FTC publishes a petition and requests comments on a proposed advertising-disclosure project for animal-derived products.

**Affected Entities**
- Consumers of advertised animal-derived products
- Restaurants, manufacturers, retailers, and other advertisers
- Food, agricultural, and advertising industries
- The FTC and overlapping regulators

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites FTC Act section 18(a)(1)(B) and Rule 1.31(f) for publishing the petition. The petition separately invokes sections 5, 12, and 15 and related advertising principles; the FTC has not adopted that merits theory.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No rule is proposed now. If later granted, the petition would require prominent disclosures across television, radio, print, digital, and other advertising about alleged health, production, welfare, and environmental concerns.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice defers merits review until after comments.
- The petition requests disclosures for all covered advertisements.
- The petition defers economic-impact information until requested.

**Inference:** The immediate decision is whether the petition warrants further action. Comment leverage is to build a record on statutory fit, substantiation, scope, alternatives, and implementation.

**Evidence Quotes**

- not intended to ... affect rulemaking proceedings
- Information will be submitted if requested
