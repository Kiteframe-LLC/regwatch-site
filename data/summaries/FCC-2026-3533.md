# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3533-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FCC proposes to permit specified part 15 devices to communicate Earth-to-space in two bands on an unprotected, non-interference basis, clarify compliant device use within spacecraft, and gather a record on space-to-Earth and other space applications.

**Affected Entities**
- satellite operators
- unlicensed-device manufacturers and users
- terrestrial licensed and unlicensed incumbents
- radio astronomy and other protected services
- small businesses and rural connectivity users
- spacecraft and ISAM operators

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is technically ambitious and potentially beneficial, but several safeguards are left for later specification.
