# Public Summary: IRS-2026-1288-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The IRS proposes section 1062 regulations for a four-year tax installment election on qualified farmland sales to qualified farmers, covering eligibility, forms, payment timing, pass-throughs, and acceleration.

**Affected Entities:** Farmland sellers and qualified farmers; pass-through entities, owners, beneficiaries, trusts, estates, corporations, transferees, and affected small entities.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites sections 1062, 7805(a), 1062(c)(2), and 1502.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal adds a 10-year covenant, Forms 1062/Schedule A, pass-through disclosures, payment and acceleration rules, and a transferee exception.

**Observed Facts**
- The election is four equal installments limited to applicable net tax liability.
- Qualified farmland generally requires United States property, a prior 10-year farming or qualifying lease period, and a 10-year covenant.
- The first installment is due by the return due date without regard to extensions.
- The RFA estimates 1.9 million potentially qualifying small entities and $237.40/8.45 hours per affected entity.

**Inference:** This is a statutory implementation rule. Comment leverage centers on operational clarity and support for the burden and small-entity conclusions.
