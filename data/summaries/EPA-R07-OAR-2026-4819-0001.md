# Public Summary: EPA-R07-OAR-2026-4819-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- EPA proposes to approve Missouri SIP revisions to 10 CSR 10-6.390.
- The revisions add a 25-ton ozone-season NOx exemption for certain spark-ignited stationary engines and make conforming reporting, recordkeeping, and cleanup edits.
- The docket includes a TSD that applies a CAA 110(l) analysis and compares Kennett emissions, the NOx SIP Call budget, and ambient monitoring data.

**Inference**
- This is a narrow SIP approval with a fairly focused air-quality showing rather than a broad policy rewrite.
- The strongest comment leverage appears to be the quality of the exemption rationale and the clarity of the remaining audit trail.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The revisions also add an exemption for certain spark-ignited internal combustion engines.
- The State's analysis supports the determination that the 25-ton NOx exemption will not affect the State's ability to meet its NOx SIP Call obligations.
- annual reporting submittals will be removed

**Affected Entities**
- Owners and operators of large stationary internal combustion engines covered by 10 CSR 10-6.390 in eastern Missouri
- Missouri Department of Natural Resources
- EPA Region 7
- Community and environmental commenters near the St. Louis ozone nonattainment area
