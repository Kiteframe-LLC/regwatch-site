# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2025-0042-0008

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- EPA announces receipt of petitions requesting new or modified pesticide-residue tolerances for specified commodities.
- The notice solicits comments before EPA responds and lists petition numbers, docket IDs, requested actions, methods, and contacts.
- The notice does not make final tolerance decisions.

**Inference:** Comment value depends on access to petition-specific summaries and supporting data in associated dockets.

**Regulatory Action:** Procedural notice of petition filings and request for comment.

**Affected Entities:** Petitioners, registrants, growers, food processors, importers, retailers, consumers, laboratories, and other parties affected by residue tolerances.

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408, especially 408(d)(2)-(4), and 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No immediate final tolerance change is made; requested changes remain under evaluation.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time or whether the data supports granting the pesticide petitions.
- The Agency is taking public comments on the requests before responding to the petitioner.
