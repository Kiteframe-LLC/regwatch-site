# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2026-3599-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA publishes notice of receipt of pesticide petitions, including IR-4's fenpropathrin request, and solicits public comment before deciding whether to establish or amend tolerances or revoke superseded tolerances.

**Affected Entities:** Food and feed producers, pesticide registrants and applicants, agricultural workers, consumers, commodity handlers, and members of the public concerned with pesticide residues or exposure.

**Statutory Authority:** Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with the petition content framework described in section 408(d)(2) and 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No tolerance is established or revoked by this document. The petition requests new or revised fenpropathrin tolerances for multiple commodity groups and revocation of existing entries where revised groupings are requested.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice lists PP 5E9210 under docket EPA-HQ-OPP-2026-3599 and identifies IR-4 as petitioner.
- The requested fenpropathrin tolerances cover 15 commodity groups or commodities, including barley, leafy greens, cherries, cottonseed, tree nuts, stone fruit, tropical fruit, turnip roots, peas, and tuberous/corm vegetables.
- The related petition summary also requests revocation of older tolerance entries because revised groupings are requested.
- Comments are due September 21, 2026, according to the notice body.

**Inference:** The most useful public comment at this stage is an evidence-focused request for EPA to test the petition's commodity coverage, residue methodology, exposure assumptions, and proposed replacement/revocation mapping before any later tolerance decision.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data at this time or whether the data supports granting the pesticide petitions.
- Additional data may be needed before EPA can make a final determination on these pesticide petitions.
- The requested tolerances are adequately supported.
