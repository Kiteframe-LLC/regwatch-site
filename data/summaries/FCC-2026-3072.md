# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3072-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts:** The NPRM would establish presumptions concerning when state and local authorization delays, fees, in-kind demands, and additional conditions for commingled facilities effectively prohibit wireline telecommunications service under Section 253.

**Inference:** The proposal seeks to convert a fact-specific statutory standard into clearer ex ante compliance benchmarks while preserving rebuttal and savings-clause arguments.

**Regulatory Action:** The FCC proposes a 120-day authorization presumption, actual-direct-cost fee limits and possible safe harbors, treatment of in-kind compensation within fee ceilings, and limits on additional requirements based on other services.

**Affected Entities**
- wireline telecommunications providers and infrastructure deployers
- state and local governments and agencies managing public rights-of-way
- small governmental jurisdictions
- residents, businesses, and communities awaiting broadband deployment
- cable franchisees and providers using commingled facilities

**Statutory Authority:** Sections 1, 4(i), 253, 303, and 403 of the Communications Act, as identified in the ordering clauses.

**Core Tradeoff:** The proposal may accelerate deployment and reduce entry barriers, but categorical presumptions could constrain legitimate local review, cost recovery, safety conditions, or varied project administration if the implementation lines are not precise.

**Evidence Quotes**
- We propose to adopt a presumption that any failure ... within 120 days ... constitutes an effective prohibition.
- actual, direct costs of managing the rights-of-way
- We tentatively conclude that the Commission possesses authority under Sections 253, 4(i), 201(b), and 303 of the Act.
