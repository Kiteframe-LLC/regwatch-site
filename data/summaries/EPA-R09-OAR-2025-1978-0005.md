# Public Summary: EPA-R09-OAR-2025-1978-0005

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve the Nevada SIP maintenance plan and PM-10 transportation budgets for 2025, 2030, and 2040 while beginning the adequacy process.

**Affected Entities:** EPA, Nevada, NNPH, Washoe County transportation agencies, the Regional Transportation Commission, residents, businesses, travelers, and people affected by PM-10 exposure.

**Statutory Authority:** CAA sections 110(k)(3), 109, 172(c)(3), 175A, and 176(c), plus 40 CFR parts 50, 52, 58, and 93.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The plan would become an approved SIP revision; the budgets would guide transportation conformity; the contingency process would operate through the maintenance period; and conformity would extend through 2040.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice reports a 2017 baseline near 10,064 lbs/day, projected totals below that baseline, three active monitors, and a 1.05 averaged-exceedance trigger.
- The proposed budgets are 4,262, 4,361, and 4,609 lbs/day for 2025, 2030, and 2040.

**Inference:** The proposal is conventional but consequential through its baseline, projections, monitoring, contingency, and conformity assumptions.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal's authority, objective, and operating mechanism fit together, but the record available here is thinner on projection uncertainty, safety-margin justification, and contingency fallback design than the approval conclusion suggests.
