# Public Summary: IRS-2026-1222-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The proposal updates section 250 deduction calculations to exclude interest and R&E expenditures, and implements section 904(b)(5) by defining which deductions may remain with foreign-source section 951A category income and reallocating specified deductions to U.S.-source income.

**Affected Entities:** Domestic corporations with foreign operations or controlled foreign corporations, and domestic corporations claiming the foreign-derived deduction eligible income deduction; their tax advisers and return preparers may also need to apply the allocation, recordkeeping, and transition rules.

**Practical Effect:** The allocation rules can change foreign tax credit limitation calculations, domestic-loss and separate-limitation-loss treatment, and the timing or sourcing of deductions for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The statutory implementation is structurally ordinary, but conclusory economic and small-entity determinations make compliance effects difficult to audit.
