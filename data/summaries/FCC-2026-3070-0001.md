# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3070-0001

## What This Document Does

**Rule Summary:** The FCC proposes a broad set of equipment-authorization controls addressing Covered List classification, white-labeling, HBOM/SBOM disclosures, covered components and software, sector-wide certification, importation and marketing, revocation, device updates, definitions, authorization term limits, SDoC registration, database modernization, submarine-cable rules, and a U.S.-based liable party.

**Policy Problem:** The FCC says recent expansion of the Covered List to production-location entries exposes gaps in equipment authorization and supply-chain visibility that may create national-security vulnerabilities.

**Affected Entities**
- Equipment manufacturers and importers
- Foreign producers and U.S. distributors
- Certification applicants, test laboratories, and Telecommunication Certification Bodies
- Online marketplaces and retailers
- UAS, router, semiconductor, software, and communications-equipment businesses
- Small entities subject to new certification, registration, and information-collection burdens
- Consumers and government users of covered equipment

**Key Mechanisms**
- HBOM/SBOM and producer disclosures with 30-day updates
- Certification requirement for devices in Covered List sectors
- Importation limits and marketing verification proposals
- Public registration of SDoC devices
- Expedited revocation with a 10-day written response
- U.S.-based liable party for foreign-granted certifications
- Definitions and term limits for equipment authorizations

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is a coherent layered control system, but broad gates may operate on incomplete supply-chain data before correction and review systems are established.
