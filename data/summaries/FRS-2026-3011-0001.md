# Public Summary: FRS-2026-3011-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Board proposes to revise 12 CFR 208.63 so Board-supervised banks must establish and maintain effective AML/CFT programs that are risk-based, include customer due diligence, and are implemented in all material respects.

**Affected Entities**
- Board-supervised banks
- Large internationally active banking organizations with distributed compliance functions
- Community banks and other smaller supervised institutions
- Bank compliance officers, boards, and senior management

**Statutory Authority:** 12 U.S.C. 1818(s), the BSA as amended by the AML Act of 2020, and the Board's delegated and independent supervisory authority.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal adds an explicit effectiveness standard, a risk-assessment requirement tied to AML/CFT Priorities, a U.S.-located responsible officer, and a maintain-by-implementation requirement.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is labeled a proposed rule and says the Board is inviting comment.
- The rule text repeatedly ties the program standard to risk-based internal policies, procedures, and controls.
- The rule text adds supervisory language about implementation 'in all material respects.'

**Inference:** This is a harmonization NPRM, but it also tightens how the Board will judge compliance and failure modes.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The proposed rule would require Board-supervised banks to establish and maintain effective AML/CFT programs.
- The rule requires implementation 'in all material respects.'

## Why It Matters
