# Public Summary: IRS-2026-1189-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Add 26 CFR 1.501(c)(3)-2 to make tax-exempt status for covered private schools conditional on not discriminating on the basis of race, color, or national or ethnic origin in education, admissions, scholarships or loans, athletics, or other school-administered or supported programs.

**Affected Entities**
- private elementary, secondary, preparatory, and post-secondary schools described in sections 501(c)(3) and 170(b)(1)(A)(ii)
- students and applicants
- families and scholarship recipients
- donors and restricted endowments
- school administrators and tax advisers
- the IRS and Treasury

**Core Tradeoff:** The proposal strengthens the tax-exemption consequence for racial nondiscrimination while potentially changing admissions, aid, donor restrictions, and school compliance practices.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal has a coherent legal structure but broad operative language and limited evidence about school-level consequences.
