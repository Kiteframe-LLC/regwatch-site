# Public Summary: OPM-2026-0661-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** RFI asking whether and how OPM should change the current FEHB and PSHB limits and structure for carrier plan options in a future rulemaking.

**Affected Entities**
- Federal employees and annuitants
- Postal Service employees and annuitants covered by PSHB
- Covered family members and other eligible participants
- FEHB and PSHB carriers
- Agency benefits officers, human-resources staff, payroll offices, and enrollment-support personnel
- OPM as program administrator and contracting authority

**Statutory Authority**
- 5 U.S.C. 8913, described as authority for regulations necessary to carry out FEHB administration
- 5 U.S.C. 8901 and related FEHB eligibility provisions
- 5 U.S.C. 8903c for the Postal Service Health Benefits Program

**Key Regulatory Change:** No regulatory text is proposed. The RFI tests whether the existing three-option or two-options-plus-HDHP structure should be changed and what evidence, safeguards, and operating conditions would be needed for a future proposal.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says current plans are limited to three options or two options and an HDHP under 5 CFR 890.201(b)(3)(i).
- The notice asks about more, fewer, or different options and separately addresses costs, consumer experience, and operations.
- The notice says the information will inform whether future notice-and-comment rulemaking is needed.

**Inference:** This is a broad record-building inquiry whose central question is whether plan-option architecture can improve value without shifting cost, complexity, or access burdens onto particular enrollee groups.

**Evidence Quotes**
- the appropriate number and distribution of options are available to enrollees
- only 66% of respondents considered it an excellent or good value
- whether modifications to the current FEHB regulations are needed through future notice and comment rulemaking
