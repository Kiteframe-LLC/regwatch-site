# Public Summary: ATF-2026-0005-0001

## What This Document Does

ATF proposes a narrow deregulatory change to 27 CFR 479.102(b)(3): makers who alter or remanufacture an existing firearm could adopt existing serial numbers or other identifying markings instead of adding a new maker mark. The rule matters because it reduces engraving burdens and costs while preserving the existing traceability trail through Form 1 and the NFRTR.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent and legally ordinary, but it rests on a narrow traceability premise and leaves small-business and administration details lightly developed.

- Gunsmiths and other marking-service providers

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Traceability rationale.** Ask ATF to show how trace workflows work when the maker mark is absent.
- **Savings range.** Ask ATF to show the effect of lower or higher engraving prices and application volumes.

- **Scope clarity.** Ask ATF for examples that make the altered-firearm exception easy to apply.
