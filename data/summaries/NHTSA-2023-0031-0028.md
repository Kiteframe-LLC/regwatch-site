# Public Summary: NHTSA-2023-0031-0028

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The SNPRM supplements the proposed Part 572 incorporation of the THOR-50M crash-test dummy by proposing an optional Humanetics four-position spine, an alternative 3D-printed face insert, and an updated upper-arm clevis, while retaining the original spine and face foam as proposed alternatives in specified configurations.

**Affected Entities:** NHTSA and its testing laboratories; vehicle manufacturers and restraint suppliers that use THOR-50M units for research, self-certification, or future compliance testing; dummy manufacturers; and technical organizations that maintain or qualify the equipment.

**Statutory Authority:** The available text identifies NHTSA's authority under its vehicle-safety and anthropomorphic-test-device authorities and proposes amendments to 49 CFR part 572. The supplied extract does not present a separate statutory-authority dispute.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposed technical package would permit a four-position spine with in-dummy data acquisition, allow a face insert as an alternative to confor foam, and update the arm-clevis specification after durability testing.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice expressly requests comments and additional data on spine equivalence, the face insert, and the proposed alternatives.
- The notice states that Kistler-spine testing was not completed because of durability issues, while the reported equivalence testing covered the Humanetics four-position spine.
- The notice expects later updates to qualification procedures and PADI materials if the four-position spine is included.

**Inference:** The most useful comments are likely technical and implementation-focused: they can improve the reproducibility, configuration control, and accessibility of the incorporated materials without treating normal solicitation of engineering data as a defect.
