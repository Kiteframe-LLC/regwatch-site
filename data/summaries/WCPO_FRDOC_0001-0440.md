# Public Summary: WCPO_FRDOC_0001-0440

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** This is a Request for Information, not a proposed regulatory text package. OWCP is asking whether and how current LHWCA hearing-loss testing standards should be updated to account for objective tests such as ABR, ASSR, and OAE while retaining accurate and fair impairment determinations.

**Affected Entities**
- Employees and claimants with LHWCA occupational hearing-loss claims
- Employers, insurance carriers, and other parties involved in LHWCA claims
- Audiologists, otolaryngologists, medical professionals, and medical associations
- Trade associations and other commenters with clinical, operational, or economic evidence
- Small medical practices and providers that may administer or interpret hearing tests
- OWCP adjudicators and other decision-makers applying hearing-loss evidence

**Statutory Authority:** The notice cites the LHWCA, 33 U.S.C. 901-950, including section 8(c)(13), 33 U.S.C. 908(c)(13), and identifies implementing regulation 20 CFR 702.441.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No immediate regulatory change is proposed in the selected notice. The inquiry could inform later changes to test administration standards, the evidentiary weight of objective tests, impairment calculations, and hearing-aid compensation questions.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice asks 28 questions across reliability and validity, clinical standards, quality control, weighting of tests, impairment calculation, economic feasibility, and OTC hearing aids.
- The notice specifically asks for peer-reviewed studies, standards, access and cost data, examples, and information about small-entity impacts.
- The notice states that qualifying audiograms have statutory presumptive status when specified conditions are met.

**Inference:** The strongest public contribution is likely to be evidence that separates clinical test validity, functional hearing loss, legal evidentiary weight, and feasible administration rather than treating objective testing as a single substitute for audiometry.

**Evidence Quotes**
- This request for information seeks the public's input on the comparative reliability and validity of audiograms versus objective tests.
- OWCP's goal is to adopt regulations that reflect current medical technology and practice while ensuring accurate, reliable, and fair determinations of hearing loss.
