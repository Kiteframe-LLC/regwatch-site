# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPP-2024-0155-0003

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA publishes a notice of receipt and request for comment on multiple pesticide petitions seeking tolerance changes, tolerance removals, regional tolerances, an import tolerance, a tolerance increase, and exemption requests.

**Affected Entities**
- Petitioners and registrants seeking pesticide tolerance changes or exemptions
- Growers, handlers, importers, and commodity groups affected by residue thresholds
- Consumers exposed through treated or imported commodities
- Analytical laboratories and compliance staff responsible for enforcement methods
- EPA staff responsible for residue chemistry, toxicology, and tolerance decisions

**Statutory Authority:** FFDCA section 408(d)(3), 21 U.S.C. 346a(d)(3), with implementing references to 40 CFR 180.7.

**Key Regulatory Change:** No final change yet; the notice opens a public record on several requested tolerance and exemption actions, including famoxadone and cymoxanil removals and new tolerances, a regional tolerance, and other petitioned actions.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice invites public comment before EPA responds to the petitioners.
- EPA says it has not fully evaluated the sufficiency of the submitted data.
- The petition list spans removals, new tolerances, an import tolerance, a tolerance increase, and exemption requests.

**Inferences**
- The most useful comments will be petition-specific and aimed at residue evidence, exposure pathways, related-commodity effects, and method validation.
- The batch format makes it harder to see which consequence belongs to which petition unless commenters and EPA separate them carefully.

**Evidence Quotes**
- notice of the receipt of and solicits public comment
- not fully evaluated the sufficiency
- may vary from that sought by the petitioner
- related to that requested commodity
- requests to amend 40 CFR 180.587
