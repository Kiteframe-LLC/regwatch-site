# Public Summary: ED-2026-OESE-1783-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Department of Education proposes to waive the general prohibition on project-period extensions involving additional Federal funds and extend one FY 2022 Arts in Education National Program grant for one more funded period through September 30, 2027.

**Affected Entities**
- The current FY 2022 84.351A grantee, John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts
- Eligible national nonprofit organizations that might otherwise seek FY 2026 Arts in Education National Program funding
- Schools, educators, and students served through the continued project activities
- Department staff responsible for developing a new priority and running a 2027 competition

**Statutory Authority:** ESEA section 4642 and the Department's cited waiver authority under GEPA and EDGAR, including the proposed waiver of 34 CFR 75.261(a) and (b)(2).

**Key Regulatory Change:** Instead of running a new FY 2026 competition, the Department would continue the current grant with additional funds for one year while it develops a new priority for a 2027 competition.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says the FY 2022 competition produced one 36-month award under ALN 84.351A for the Kennedy Center National Arts Education Program.
- The notice says the Department already used a proposed and final waiver in 2025 to extend the same project's period through September 30, 2026.
- The notice says the new continuation award would be at an amount consistent with the FY 2025 extension.

**Inference:** This is a continuity-focused bridge proposal. The strongest comment angles concern whether the Department has shown necessity, bounded duration, and an adequate explanation for postponing competition rather than how the ongoing arts activities themselves are designed.

**Evidence Quotes**
- there is not enough time in FY 2026 to develop a new priority and carry out a full, robust competitive process
- The waiver will allow the Department to issue a continuation award in FY 2026
- one additional period, not to exceed September 30, 2027
