# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OAR-2025-1348-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes a technology-review/reconsideration rule for the Oil and Gas NESHAP covering subparts HH and HHH, including no revision for existing standards, a new methanol standard for regulated production-facility emission points, two alternative approaches for previously unregulated emission points, a major-source-definition revision for production facilities, and minor technical corrections.

**Affected Entities**
- Crude oil and natural gas production facilities
- Natural gas transmission and storage facilities
- Natural gas processing plants
- Owners and operators of controlled equipment such as dehydrators, storage vessels, controllers, pumps, AGRUs, and loading operations
- State, local, Tribal, and delegated air agencies that will administer the final standards

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is labeled a proposed rule and gives a public comment deadline of June 22, 2026.
- EPA states that it is not proposing revisions to the current standards for the already regulated emission points.
- EPA states that it is proposing new standards for previously unregulated emission points under an alternative path and also proposing a narrower no-obligation path.
- The notice separately proposes a change to the major-source definition for production facilities by removing the exception for glycol dehydrators and storage vessels.

**Inference**
- The proposal is not a single incremental cleanup rule; it is a branching NPRM with a statutory-interpretation question at its center.
- The most productive comments are likely to focus on the new standards' baseline evidence, the no-obligation theory, and the source-counting consequences of the associated-equipment change.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is further proposing two alternative approaches to emission points not currently regulated in these NESHAP.
- The EPA is also concurrently proposing changes to the definition of “associated equipment” with respect to a major source to align with the CAA.
