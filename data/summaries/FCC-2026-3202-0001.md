# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3202-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The FCC proposes to reconsider the scope and statutory interpretation of E-Rate, seek policy options for child protection and screen time, revise CIPA implementation questions, strengthen program-integrity controls including consultant registration and certifications, streamline administration, and remove specified Emergency Connectivity Fund rules.

**Affected Entities**
- Schools and school districts
- Libraries and library systems
- Students, parents, guardians, and teachers
- E-Rate service providers and consultants
- USAC and FCC program administrators

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies principally on Communications Act section 254(h), section 254(c)(1), and CIPA, while acknowledging uncertainty about the scope of authority to narrow or sunset E-Rate.

**Main Requirements**
- Potentially narrow, sunset, or reorient E-Rate support.
- Potentially revise interpretation and implementation of CIPA.
- Require consultant registration, training, certifications, conflict disclosures, and non-percentage fee arrangements.
- Remove identified ECF provisions and revise administration and integrity rules.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice identifies the proposed subjects and includes regulatory text for consultant controls and ECF removals.

**Inference:** The proposal is a multi-track rulemaking; each track has different evidence, statutory, and implementation questions.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal has concrete integrity mechanisms but the available record does not fully connect distinct problems to calibrated remedies and correction paths.
