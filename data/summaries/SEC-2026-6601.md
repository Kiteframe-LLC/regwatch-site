# Public Summary: SEC-2026-6601-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The SEC is publishing a section 610 review list and inviting evidence about whether the 2017 exhibit-hyperlink and HTML-format filing requirements should continue unchanged, be amended, or be rescinded. The notice itself does not amend the underlying requirements.

**Affected Entities:** Small registrants and other small entities subject to the cited exhibit and filing provisions, their filing and compliance staff, outside filing vendors, investors and public users who rely on accessible filings, and the SEC staff responsible for the later review.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice relies on section 610 of the Regulatory Flexibility Act, 5 U.S.C. 610, within the RFA provisions codified at 5 U.S.C. 601 through 612, and identifies the securities-law authorities cited for the underlying 2017 rule.

**Key Regulatory Changes**
- No operative regulatory change is made by this notice.
- The SEC identifies the 2017 Exhibit Hyperlinks and HTML Format rule for section 610 review.
- The SEC invites comments on continuation, amendment, or rescission and on whether small-business effects are new or different from those at adoption.
- The listed rule requires hyperlinks to exhibits and HTML-format submission for specified filings.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice identifies the rule's affected CFR provisions as 17 CFR 229.601, 232.11, 232.102, 232.105, 239.40, and 249.20f.
- The notice states that the rule was adopted on March 1, 2017.
- The notice cites a 2017 Final Regulatory Flexibility Analysis and a 2016 Initial Regulatory Flexibility Analysis.
- The notice does not provide current entity counts, cost estimates, complaint data, or a rule-specific comparison of continuation, amendment, and rescission.

**Inference:** The document opens a record-building stage for a narrow technology-and-filing obligation, with the central tradeoff being reliable, usable public disclosure against the cost and operational burden of producing and maintaining compliant HTML filings and exhibit links.
