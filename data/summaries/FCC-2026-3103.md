# Public Summary: FCC-2026-3103-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Amend the Table of TV Allotments in 47 CFR § 73.622(j) by replacing Elko, Nevada's UHF channel 20 entry with VHF channel 10.

**Affected Entities:** Reno (KENV-TV) Licensee, Inc.; KENV-DT; viewers in and around Elko; and parties interested in broadcast allotment and interference issues.

**Statutory Authority:** The notice lists 47 U.S.C. §§ 154, 155, 301, 303, 307, 309, 310, 334, 336, and 339 as authority.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The Table entry changes from channel 20 to channel 10 using the station's current licensed parameters.

**Observed Facts**
- The Licensee holds a construction permit (CP) to construct a facility on UHF channel 20 at Elko, and now requests that the Bureau substitute VHF channel 10 for UHF channel 20 in the Table with technical parameters set forth in KENV-DT's current license.
- The notice identifies § 73.618(a) as the principal community coverage requirement.

**Inference:** The proposal is intended to preserve the station's ability to remain on the air while changing its allotted channel; the supplied synopsis does not independently establish the full engineering basis.
