# Public Summary: CMS-2026-2081-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** RFI asking whether CMS should revise the EHB framework, the typical employer plan standard, State benchmark selection methods, review frequency, and the State update process.

**Affected Entities**
- individual and small-group consumers
- health insurers and exchange issuers
- State insurance regulators and Medicaid/Exchange administrators
- employers whose plans are used as typicality references
- brokers, consultants, and other market analysts who may submit data or testimony

**Observed Facts**
- The notice expressly states that CMS seeks public input on the EHB framework and typical employer plan standard.
- The notice organizes questions into seven topical areas covering typicality, State variation, affordability, scope of benefits, review frequency, State update processes, and implementation effects.
- The document does not propose specific regulatory text; it seeks evidence, alternatives, and analytical inputs for future rulemaking.

**Inference:** The main value of public comments is to define better data sources, clarify what a 'typical employer plan' should mean, and test how any later EHB changes might affect access, costs, and State flexibility.

**Evidence Quotes**
- comprehensive review of the Essential Health Benefits framework
- scope of EHB be equal to the scope of benefits
- variation in the scope of benefits included as EHB
- potential future policy changes
