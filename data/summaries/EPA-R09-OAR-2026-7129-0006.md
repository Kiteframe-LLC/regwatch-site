# Public Summary: EPA-R09-OAR-2026-7129-0006

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve California's Coachella Valley Extreme-area RFP demonstration, emissions inventories, and 2023-2031 motor-vehicle emissions budgets for the 2008 ozone NAAQS.

**Affected Entities**
- California, CARB, SCAQMD, and Riverside County
- Transportation agencies, SCAG, FHWA, and FTA using the budgets for conformity
- Sources represented in the VOC and NO X inventories
- Residents and communities affected by ozone precursor emissions

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 110(k)(3), 172(c)(2)-(3), 176(c), 182(b)(1), 182(c)(2)(B)-(C), and the implementing regulations cited in 40 CFR parts 51 and 93.

**Key Regulatory Change:** Final approval would add the RFP elements and Table 5 budgets to the California SIP and replace the prior adequate budgets for transportation conformity.

**Core Tradeoff:** The proposal supplies a usable technical plan, but the prepared packet does not include the underlying model inputs, appendices, or substitution calculations.

**Observed Facts**
- The plan uses a 2011 baseline and projects through 2031.
- Table 4 reports no RFP shortfall using NO X reductions for VOC substitution beginning in 2017.
- Table 5 lists declining VOC and NO X budgets for 2023, 2026, 2029, and 2031; other Extreme-area elements are deferred.

**Inference:** The highest-value comment path is a reproducibility request: require EPA to show how the baseline inventory, NO X substitution, RFP milestones, and conformity budgets fit together before final approval.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The EPA is proposing to approve the RFP demonstration and associated emissions inventory and budgets.
- The EPA has not approved an Extreme area attainment demonstration for the Coachella Valley for the 2008 ozone NAAQS.
