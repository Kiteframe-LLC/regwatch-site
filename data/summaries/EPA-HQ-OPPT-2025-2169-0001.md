# Public Summary: EPA-HQ-OPPT-2025-2169-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a proposed rule with a live public-comment deadline and a Federal Register publication date of June 5, 2026.
- EPA says the SNURs follow prior TSCA Orders and are tied to the conditions imposed in those Orders.
- The preamble says EPA evaluated SNUN reporting costs and export-notification costs, and points readers to docketed support materials.
- The proposal retains TSCA recordkeeping requirements and export/import compliance hooks.

**Inference**
- This is a program-wide SNUR package, not a housekeeping amendment, because it changes the timing and scope of when covered chemical activities may start across multiple compliance paths.

## Who Is Affected

- Manufacturers and processors of one or more subject chemical substances
- Importers and exporters subject to TSCA certification and export-notification rules
- State, local, and Tribal permitting authorities that will have to interpret the scope of the SNURs

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Issue:** Explain how implementation stays consistent across programs.
  **Why it matters:** A multi-program rule can become uneven if delegated authorities apply the carveout differently.
  **Ask the agency to:** publish model language or examples for SIP, PSD, NNSR, and Tribal NSR authorities.
  **Evidence to attach or request:** The proposal applies across multiple NSR programs, but the visible text does not give model examples.
- **Issue:** Show the burden math and the boundary examples.
  **Why it matters:** The proposal depends on both the cost story and the carveout line-drawing.
  **Ask the agency to:** provide the docketed burden analysis and a concrete example set.
  **Evidence to attach or request:** The visible preamble gives summary cost figures and a broad carveout rationale, but not the intermediate reasoning.
- **Issue:** Map the SNUR to the underlying TSCA Orders.
  **Why it matters:** Commenters need to know what is inherited from the Orders and what is new in this rulemaking.
  **Ask the agency to:** provide a side-by-side mapping of the restrictions.
  **Evidence to attach or request:** The preamble says the SNURs track the Orders, but the visible record does not fully map the two together.
- **Issue:** Clarify the public-record trail for CBI-heavy substances.
  **Why it matters:** Public review gets harder when substance identities and supporting materials are partially sanitized.
  **Ask the agency to:** explain what traceability remains available to the public and what sits only in confidential channels.
  **Evidence to attach or request:** The docket contains many supporting PMNs and sanitized materials, which makes the traceability question important.
