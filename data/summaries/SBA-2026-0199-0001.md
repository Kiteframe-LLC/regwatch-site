# Public Summary: SBA-2026-0199-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Revise SBA's small-business size standards for 338 NAICS industry groups and industries, including receipt, employee, asset, and capacity-based thresholds.

**Affected Entities**
- small businesses near current or proposed thresholds
- firms seeking Federal contracts
- SBA loan applicants
- Federal contracting officers and agencies
- small-business program administrators
- industries receiving lower or unchanged effective thresholds

**Core Tradeoff:** Higher thresholds may improve growth runway and broaden eligibility, but may also increase competition from larger firms for programs intended for small businesses and may make industry comparisons less transparent.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is structurally coherent, with its main weakness being incomplete inspectability of the incorporated methodology and aggregate impact claims.
