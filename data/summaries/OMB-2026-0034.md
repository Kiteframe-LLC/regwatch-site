# Public Summary: OMB-2026-0034-0001

## What This Document Does

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is a proposed rule that would revise the government-wide guidance for federal financial assistance and require conforming agency changes.
- The proposal packages subrecipient oversight, payment justification, termination and suspension, national policy, domestic preference, program integrity, and publication-cost changes.
- The record includes a supporting RIA and a separate RFA small-entity analysis.

**Inference**
- This is a broad cross-government grant-policy overhaul rather than a narrow technical amendment.
- The proposal will matter most to grantmaking agencies, recipients, subrecipients, and compliance staff who must operationalize the changes across multiple programs.

**Evidence Quotes**
- OMB proposes to revise several parts of the OMB Guidance for Federal Financial Assistance located in title 2 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), subtitle A.
- The listed Federal agencies also propose conforming changes to their respective implementing regulations.
