# Public Summary: EPA-R05-OAR-2025-0259-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Approve Illinois SIP revisions in 35 IAC parts 217, 218, and 219 as meeting specified 2015-ozone Moderate VOC/NOx and Serious NOx RACT requirements, including negative declarations and a source-specific permit pathway.

**Affected Entities**
- Illinois EPA and regulated stationary sources
- major VOC and NOx sources in the Chicago and Metro-East nonattainment areas
- sources covered by the listed CTGs
- nearby residents and communities affected by ozone-forming emissions
- EPA and state air-quality enforcement officials

**Core Tradeoff:** The proposal recognizes existing and flexible state controls as sufficient, potentially reducing duplicative regulation, while relying on the quality of state inventories, technical comparisons, permit enforceability, and averaging safeguards.

**Observed Fact:** The notice proposes approval and identifies the statutes, regulations, source categories, negative declarations, and permit at issue.

**Inference:** The practical consequence is to make the submitted state framework federally enforceable for the addressed RACT obligations.

## Why It Matters
