# Public Summary: EPA-R09-OAR-2026-5743-0002

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** Approve Rule 352 and conditionally approve Rule 353 into the Arizona SIP, along with related RACT demonstrations.

**Affected Entities**
- Maricopa County gasoline cargo-tank owners and operators
- Gasoline dispensing facilities
- MCAQD, ADEQ, and EPA
- Residents of the Phoenix-Mesa ozone nonattainment area

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 110, 110(k), 110(l), 110(k)(4), and 182(b)(2).

**Key Regulatory Change:** The revised local rules would replace current SIP-approved versions; Rule 353 would remain contingent on a one-year corrective commitment.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal addresses SIP enforceability and ozone controls through a conventional approval and conditional-approval framework.
