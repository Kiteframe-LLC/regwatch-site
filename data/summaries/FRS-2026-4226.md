# Public Summary: FRS-2026-4226-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** The Board proposes Regulation UU in 12 CFR part 247 and related amendments to implement its GENIUS Act responsibilities for Board-supervised permitted payment stablecoin issuers, covered custodians, State-qualified PPSIs, and the tying prohibition applicable to all PPSIs.

**Affected Entities**
- Board-supervised PPSIs, including subsidiaries of insured State member banks
- State-qualified PPSIs subject to the Board's backup or transition authority
- Banks, holding companies, and other Board-supervised covered custodians
- Payment stablecoin holders, digital-asset service providers, and other market participants
- State member banks considering a PPSI subsidiary and small entities that may provide related services

**Statutory Authority:** The GENIUS Act, 12 U.S.C. 5901 et seq., including sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, and 16, with related amendments under the Federal Reserve Act, the Bank Holding Company Act, HOLA, and the Board's existing capital and procedural rules.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposal would define the Board's stablecoin framework; restrict activities and remuneration; require one-to-one segregated reserves, monthly disclosure, examination, and certification; establish redemption, risk-management, reporting, capital, custody, tying, backup-enforcement, and transition rules; and align related banking regulations.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice is labeled a notice of proposed rulemaking and requests comments on all aspects of the proposal.
- The rule covers reserve assets, redemption, risk management, audits and supervision, capital, custody, tying, State-qualified PPSIs, and related amendments.
- The economic analysis states that the market is nascent, that no PPSIs currently operate under a uniform Federal framework, and that the analysis is mostly qualitative.
- The regulatory analysis includes PRA and IRFA discussions and identifies estimated reporting costs per Board-supervised PPSI.

**Inference:** This is a broad implementing NPRM rather than a narrow technical rule because it creates an integrated compliance architecture across issuance, reserves, custody, capital, and supervision.

**Evidence Quotes**
- The proposal implements the Board's responsibilities under the GENIUS Act.
- A Board-supervised PPSI must maintain reserve assets that at all times have a total fair value that equals or exceeds the outstanding issuance value.
- The subsequent discussion is mostly qualitative in nature.

## Why It Matters

Core structural concern: The proposal is legally grounded and structurally coherent, but its broad principles-based framework is calibrated against a market the Board says is nascent and data-limited.
