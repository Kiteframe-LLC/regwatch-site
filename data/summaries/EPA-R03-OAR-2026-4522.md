# Public Summary: EPA-R03-OAR-2026-4522-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** EPA proposes to approve DCDOEE's negative declaration for existing OSWI in the District of Columbia and amend 40 CFR part 62 to reflect that no state plan is required for the category while the no-source finding remains correct.

**Affected Entities:** DCDOEE, EPA Region III, current and future District combustion-unit applicants or operators, permitting staff, and communities concerned about solid-waste-incineration emissions.

**Statutory Authority:** Clean Air Act sections 111(d) and 129, with the notice citing 40 CFR 60.23(b), 60.23a(b), 62.06, and the OSWI definitions in 40 CFR part 60.

**Key Regulatory Change:** The proposed amendment records the negative declaration in part 62 rather than imposing operating, monitoring, or reporting requirements on an identified source.

**Observed Facts**
- The notice says DCDOEE submitted the declaration on May 21, 2026.
- The supporting letter is dated May 1, 2026 and states that DOEE reviewed District sources and found no qualifying OSWI.
- The notice identifies subpart FFFF, while the supporting letter refers to subpart EEEE.

**Inference:** The legal route is conventional, but the proposal's factual predicate is less auditable than it should be and the subpart inconsistency is material until corrected.

**Core Tradeoff:** Avoid an unnecessary state plan if no covered source exists while preserving reliable category identification, public accountability, and a workable future-source pathway.

**Evidence Quotes**
- EPA is proposing to approve the negative declaration submitted by the District of Columbia Department of Energy and Environment.
- The negative declaration satisfies the requirements of 40 CFR 60.23(b) and 62.06.
- The District of Columbia ... hereby submits a negative declaration in lieu of a state plan ... for OSWI units subject to 40 CFR 60 Subpart EEEE.
