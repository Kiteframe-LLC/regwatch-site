# Public Summary: CPSC-2026-0166-0001

## What This Document Does

**Regulatory Action:** OMB and many grantmaking agencies propose to revise title 2 CFR financial-assistance rules, clarify that OMB's subtitle A policies are regulations, change numerous part 200 grant-management requirements, remove or revise outdated provisions, and have agencies including CPSC adopt the revised OMB regulation in subtitle B.

**Affected Entities**
- Federal grantmaking agencies
- States, local governments, and Tribes
- Universities, nonprofits, and other recipients or subrecipients
- CPSC financial-assistance recipients

**Main Requirements**
- Clarify that OMB subtitle A grants policies operate as regulation.
- Revise part 200 grant-management rules on payments, disclosures, procurement, termination, national policy provisions, and agency responsibilities.
- Create CPSC part 6100 adopting OMB part 200 for CPSC awards.

## Issues Worth Commenting On (0-6)

- **Issue:** The CPSC docket should make the controlling OMB docket and RIA easy to find.
  
  **Why It Matters:** The notice directs comments to OMB-2026-0034 for the full text, so readers of the CPSC docket may miss the main record.
  
  **Practical Question:** Will comments filed in an agency-specific docket be considered, and where is the RIA linked?
- **Issue:** The burden-reduction claim needs quantified support.
  
  **Why It Matters:** Recipients may lose some policy add-ons but gain new payment, disclosure, and oversight duties.
  
  **Practical Question:** What are the expected net costs by recipient class, award type, and implementation year?
- **Issue:** Major controls should be compared to narrower alternatives.
  
  **Why It Matters:** Uniform rules can improve oversight but may overburden low-risk awards if not tailored.
  
  **Practical Question:** Why not use thresholds, pilots, enhanced documentation, or risk-based exceptions for some changes?
- **Issue:** CPSC should identify transition effects for its own award programs.
  
  **Why It Matters:** The proposed CPSC part is short and does not explain whether existing or future CPSC awards need exceptions or timing rules.
  
  **Practical Question:** Which CPSC assistance programs are affected, and are any agency-specific clarifications needed?
